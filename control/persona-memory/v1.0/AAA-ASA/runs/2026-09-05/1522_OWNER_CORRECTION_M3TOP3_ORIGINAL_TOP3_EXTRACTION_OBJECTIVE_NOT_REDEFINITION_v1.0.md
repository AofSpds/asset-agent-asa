## Owner correction
The prior ASA wording that the project objective was being "redefined" as a conditional Top3 selection/filtering model was inaccurate.

M3Top3 was originally conceived and titled around Top3 extraction. The persistent model review memory already describes M3Top3 as `3M Opportunity Discovery / cross-sectional ranker` with `Top3 primary + Model Top10 diagnostic` and `3M MFE Rank` as the primary opportunity-discovery Ground Truth.

Therefore this is not a new model-purpose change. It is a restoration/clarification of the original research objective.

## Original research objective to preserve
At each historical/current snapshot, identify the three most suitable opportunity candidates under the then-current market / industry / company conditions.

The research objective is not exhaustive perfect reconstruction or perfect prediction for every member of the universe. Universe, eligibility, evidence, PIT, and feature controls are supporting mechanisms for a trustworthy Top3 extraction process.

## Consequences
- `M3Top3` remains a conditional cross-sectional Top3 opportunity-selection/ranking model.
- The primary research question is whether the model can surface the best three opportunity candidates under the information set available at the snapshot.
- Hard eligibility should remove genuinely out-of-scope / non-tradable / otherwise proven-ineligible companies, not companies merely because evidence is incomplete.
- Evidence confidence, eligibility uncertainty, and feature missingness should be tracked separately from economic eligibility where feasible.
- Exhaustive completion of every historical field/company-window is not a default objective if it does not materially improve Top3 identification reliability.
- Data collection should increasingly be prioritized by expected information value for Top3 selection, critical-miss prevention, ranking stability, and evidence safety.
- Existing PIT/outcome firewall remains in force; future outcomes must not be used to fabricate or retrospectively choose same-replay historical inputs.
- This correction does not itself authorize any model weight/feature/scorer/PIT semantic change.

## Current PC1 effect
The in-flight PC1 process-calibration run continues without restart. Its telemetry remains useful because it measures the cost/yield of evidence acquisition and feature materialization. Post-PC1 tuning should evaluate source routes by their contribution to reliable Top3 discrimination, not by exhaustive document completion alone.
