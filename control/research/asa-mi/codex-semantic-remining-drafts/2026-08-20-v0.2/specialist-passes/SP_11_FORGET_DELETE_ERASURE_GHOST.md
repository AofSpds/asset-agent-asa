## Recovered distinctions

“Forget” may denote activation decay, retrieval suppression, scope-specific inaccessibility, dormancy, supersession, compression, archival, logical deletion, physical erasure, or model unlearning. “Delete” also has different targets: locator, relation, source bytes, normalized object, derived view, heuristic, self-model effect, learned parameter, Authority binding, and downstream real-world consequence.

The sources jointly reject two symmetric errors:

- retaining a fully retrievable dossier while calling it forgotten;
- treating deletion of one source record as proof that its causal influence disappeared.

An erasure receipt therefore needs a declared target and criterion. Byte absence, retrieval failure, dependency invalidation, and counterfactual behavioral non-influence are progressively stronger and are not interchangeable. Some effects cannot be rolled back (executed actions, other agents' copies, a human's changed knowledge), so “complete erasure” may be incoherent without a bounded system boundary.

## Relations recovered

1. Functional forgetting `ALTERNATIVE_TO` content deletion for some purposes.
2. Privacy erasure `IS_NOT_SATISFIED_BY` salience decay alone.
3. Source deletion `DOES_NOT_IMPLY` deletion of derived influence.
4. Dependency invalidation `MAY_WEAKEN` ghost influence.
5. State restoration `DOES_NOT_UNDO` external side effects.
6. Supersession `PRESERVES` history while changing active use.
7. Deletion authority `IS_SEPARATE_FROM` memory content and access.
8. Erasure verification `DEPENDS_ON` declared boundary and causal criterion.

## Adversarial cases

- A deleted episode survives in a summary, heuristic, embedding, cache, fine-tune, or relationship model.
- All derived records are deleted, yet prior decisions altered the environment that now drives behavior.
- Retrieval is suppressed for ordinary tasks but remains available to audit or legal processes.
- A superseded harmful rule remains too salient and continues to influence ranking.
- Deleting corrective history resurrects an older error through remaining derived state.

## Materiality judgment

No new source object is counted. The pass makes explicit that deletion success is relative to target, boundary, and causal criterion; it also preserves the sources' distinction between reversible state and irreversible downstream consequence.
