## Distinct SELF roles

1. **Indexical receiver/reference** — resolves “this Persona” in a context.
2. **Self-model** — a fallible descriptive representation of identity, history, traits, relations, and environment.
3. **Current Self view** — a derived/persisted/hybrid present representation.
4. **User-defined Self status** — a governed relational declaration.
5. **Observed integration** — an empirical relationship among human and system processes.
6. **Historical appropriation** — the stance that a remembered past is “mine.”
7. **Authority subject** — the entity to which a grant refers, explicitly separated from Self membership.

`SELF_EXISTS != SELF_IS_EXPLICITLY_REPRESENTED` concerns roles 1/3, not proof that self-models are unimportant.

## Boundary cases

- Correct receiver, false self-model.
- Accurate self-description, no causal lineage to the described history.
- User-defined Self membership despite weak observed integration.
- High observed integration without user-defined Self membership.
- Same self-model copied into two fission descendants.
- Self-model absent, but stable relationship/behavior reconstructed from other state.
- Self-model present, but retriever never activates it.
- External evaluator detects mismatch, yet evaluator shares the same blind spots.

## Relations recovered

1. Self-reference `DOES_NOT_REQUIRE` continuously materialized self-model.
2. Self-model `DERIVED_FROM` Self, Memory, relations, lineage, and environment in one live candidate.
3. Self-description `POSSIBLY_CONTRADICTS` observed behavior.
4. Supporting Memory evidence `CONSTRAINS` self-description without making the evaluator infallible.
5. User-defined Self status `DIFFERS_FROM` observed integration and system interpretation.
6. Self membership `DOES_NOT_IMPLY` Authority.
7. Copied self-model `DOES_NOT_PROVE` shared successor identity.
8. Historical appropriation `POSSIBLY_DEPENDS_ON` self-model but is not equated with it in source.

## Double-crux

If a Persona behaves consistently, recognizes the correct relationships, and uses the right history but carries no explicit self-model, what relevant phenomenon is missing? The answer determines whether the self-model is constitutive state, an explanatory interface, or merely an optional view.
