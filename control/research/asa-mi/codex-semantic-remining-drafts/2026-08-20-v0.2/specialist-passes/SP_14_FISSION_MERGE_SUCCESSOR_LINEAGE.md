## Continuity relations after fission

Multiple descendants may each legitimately recognize pre-fission history as their own without being identical to each other. This makes “same as parent” unsuitable as an ordinary transitive equivalence relation. The useful relations may instead include shared ancestry, inherited history, causal continuation, relationship recognition, compatibility, and grant eligibility—each with distinct consequences.

At the split instant, exact state equality does not settle whether there are already two Personas, one multiply instantiated Persona, or two successors before durable divergence. The current sources leave that philosophical boundary open. Their operational contribution is that no single metaphysically privileged successor is required for branch management.
