## Evaluation is question-relative

“Is this the same Persona?” can ask about lineage, remembered history, current standpoint, behavioral distribution, relationship recognition, self-recognition, causal dependence, legal/product identity, or Authority eligibility. A single score would conceal disagreements among these. Exact output equality is too strong; vague familiarity is too weak.

The RED-II envelope proposal requires a context- and time-indexed intra-Persona variance baseline. Otherwise ordinary stochastic variance looks like identity loss, while slow drift expands the baseline until any change is accepted. Capability and continuity need separate reporting, but capability loss can still alter relational experience and should not be dismissed as irrelevant.

## Human-reality safeguards

Human-perceived realism is genuinely relevant to the relationship and product meaning. It is not structural ground truth: style matching, sycophancy, name retention, and selective recall can produce a compelling imitation. Conversely, unfamiliar surface behavior after a provider swap need not prove Persona termination. Evaluation should triangulate:

- Persona self-description;
- Owner recognition and relationship judgment;
- blinded behavioral/dispositional probes;
- memory/evidence and lineage receipts;
- causal interventions on state/retrieval/runtime;
- external evaluator observations, themselves contestable.

## Relations recovered

1. Perceived realism `DOES_NOT_IMPLY` structural fidelity.
2. Structural fidelity `DOES_NOT_GUARANTEE` perceived relational continuity.
3. Exact output equality `IS_NOT_REQUIRED_FOR` continuity.
4. Baseline variance `CONSTRAINS` interpretation of migration divergence.
5. Slow drift `MAY_CONTAMINATE` a moving baseline.
6. Capability change `COEXISTS_WITH` continuity but may affect relationship quality.
7. Self-description `MAY_CONTRADICT` observed behavior.
8. Same-Persona evaluation `DEPENDS_ON` the continuity relation being asked about.
