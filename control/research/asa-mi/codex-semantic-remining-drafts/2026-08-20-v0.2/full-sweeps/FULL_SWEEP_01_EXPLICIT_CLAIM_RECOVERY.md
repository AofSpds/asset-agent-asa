## Source-faithful claim map

1. The historical normalized layer is secondary evidence. It explicitly prohibits upgrading source-derived objects into current Owner position, Requirement, validation result, or production authority.
2. The historical Owner proposition is the deliberately strong `Identity ?= Memory`; the same source says it is unconfirmed and does not assert a scientifically proven human ontology.
3. RED-I rejects only a literal strong equation and preserves Memory as a major continuity carrier. Its alternatives range from necessity-without-sufficiency to multi-dimensional continuity and versioned process.
4. Process discontinuity is a native target condition in the live worldview. This is not a claim that reconstruction is invariant to retriever, compiler, model, runtime, or environment.
5. `Persona State is not a memory dump` constrains, but does not by itself refute, later broad Memory candidates. The unresolved term is what constitutes Memory rather than whether state has multiple representations.
6. Historical `MEMORY != CURRENT STATE` is a real losing/historical position. The live corpus later treats Current Status as stored, derived, cached, or hybrid and treats `CURRENT` as an operator; the old firewall must remain visible.
7. Historical event/evidence, episodic representation, interpretation, derived lesson, and present effect are distinct. The source family consistently resists latest-summary replacement of history.
8. Canonical bytes, canonical evidence, canonical semantic state, derived index, and materialized current view are not interchangeable. Rebuildability is a hypothesis because an index may contain irrecoverable judgment.
9. The reconstruction stack is causally plural: persistent state, retrieval, context compilation, model prior, runtime configuration, active context, tools, and environment can each alter current behavior.
10. Provider replacement is a design intent. Behavioral compatibility, expertise portability, relationship continuity, and full provider independence are explicitly unproven or definition-dependent.
11. A weak local core may preserve bounded continuity without cloud parity, but may be unable to semantically supervise stronger external cognition. Bounded enforcement and full semantic review are different candidates.
12. Common Memory is not a single proposal. The source distinguishes shared evidence/history from shared interpretation, with the latter creating convergence and correlated-error risk.
13. Forgetting decomposes into salience/non-retrieval, dormancy, supersession, compression, archival, deletion, and cryptographic erasure. Source deletion can leave derived influence.
14. Fission can be operationally coherent without one metaphysically unique successor. Merge remains union, reconciliation, or new-successor alternatives; no source makes Merge mandatory.
15. Memory, capability, expertise, intimacy, self-membership, and continuity do not automatically confer Authority. Authority can nevertheless become state-sensitive when an old grant's assumptions no longer hold.
16. Origin and derivation are separate. Repetition and summarization can launder provenance without changing where a claim began.
17. Per-write safety does not cover compositional drift. Slow trajectories can cross a material boundary even when no single write does.
18. Audit independence depends on roots and error correlation, not a role label. Control safety must tolerate Audit failure.
19. Human-perceived realism is relevant but not sufficient; it is explicitly distinct from structural fidelity and from ontology.
20. Mature CS abstractions are a starting prior, not reality. Context, identity, reference, cache, versioning, serialization, provenance, and lifecycle should receive prior-art mapping before invention, while Persona-specific abstraction loss remains research.
