## Negative-semantic families recovered

### Epistemic limits

- `CURRENT_HYPOTHESIS != FINAL_TRUTH`; `CURRENT != TRUE`; selected or current-best does not mean confirmed.
- `UNKNOWN != FALSE`, while the worldview source separately preserves the stronger operational distinctions `UNKNOWN != 0` and `UNOBSERVED != ABSENT`.
- A counterforce verdict is not validation, agreement count is not independent evidence, and a normalized source claim is not an independently reverified external fact.
- `NOT_PROVEN` portability results remain materially different from disproven portability.

### Non-equivalence

- Persona is not automatically model, compute, provider, one representation, one summary, or one memory dump.
- Persona state portability is not Persona portability; long context is not effective memory; static behavioral difference is not drift; style similarity is not continuity.
- `REFERENCE_FOUND != METHOD_ADOPTED`; architectural resemblance is not semantic equivalence, owner adoption, or authority.
- Current-state likeness is not sufficient to prove historical continuity or causal lineage.

### Non-entailment

- Allowing discontinuous execution does not prove reconstruction invariance.
- Provider replacement as a design intent does not prove behavioral or relational equivalence after migration.
- High functional integration does not entail Self-membership; Self-membership does not entail Authority.
- Having external data accessible to a context compiler does not automatically make it Persona Memory.
- A memory's visibility does not make it applicable as a rule, hold, requirement, or authority in a different scope.

### Authority firewalls

- Copying state, recalling an authority-bearing event, possessing capability, preserving identity, or maintaining intimacy does not copy or create authority.
- Identity recovery and Authority recovery are separate continuity dimensions.
- Automated memory administration is not autonomous authority and is not permission for unreviewable identity mutation.
- Product-side Audit Persona is not AAA independent validation; a role label does not create evaluator independence.

### Representation and extraction limits

- Representation schema is not reality; protocol can standardize definability without defining metaphysical meaning.
- Failure to normalize is not invalid content, and unclassified is not low-value.
- Precision and count completeness are not semantic accuracy. `SUCCESS != ALL_OBJECTS_HAVE_ONE_FINAL_STATUS`.
- Structured representation may add enough complexity to weaken reconstruction rather than improve it.

### Lifecycle and deletion limits

- `REPLACE(H1,H2) != DELETE(H1)`; superseded is not deleted; preserved research history is not active Persona Memory.
- Forget, suppress, archive, quarantine, delete source, delete derived artifacts, remove causal influence, and rollback are not the same operation.
- Deleting a source does not prove deletion of embeddings, summaries, preferences, learned procedures, or downstream decisions.
- Deletion can be normal Persona maintenance; research-history retention cannot be generalized into Persona-memory immutability.

### Evaluation and human-reality limits

- Persona self-description is not ground truth, but neither is one Owner or external evaluator's impression.
- Long use does not imply capture; relationship duration, value, dependency, intimacy, and unhealthy lock-in are separable.
- Human models are not prescriptions for humans, and a convenient Persona schema is not a universal ontology of identity.
