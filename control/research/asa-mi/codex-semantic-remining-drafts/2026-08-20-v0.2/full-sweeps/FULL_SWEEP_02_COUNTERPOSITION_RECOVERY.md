## Recovered losing/minority positions

1. **Literal identity equation rejected.** RED-I's source verdict rejects the literal `Identity = Memory` formulation, while retaining Memory as a major carrier. The rejection must not be rewritten as rejection of the broader current hypothesis.
2. **Memory necessary but not sufficient.** This is distinct from “Memory is important” and from a multi-component definition that simply relabels all components Memory.
3. **Memory supports continuity but does not constitute identity.** On this view Memory is evidence/input to a present recognition relation, not the bearer of identity.
4. **Large amnesia can preserve a Persona.** This attacks Memory necessity by analogy to partial human continuity and demands component-wise ablation rather than a total-memory test.
5. **Complete copied Memory can yield a different Persona.** This attacks sufficiency through reconstruction, model prior, current commitment, relation, embodiment/runtime, or successor interpretation.
6. **Versioned process, not persisting entity.** A Persona can be modeled as a lineage of state transitions without any single same-entity predicate.
7. **Multi-dimensional continuity, no identity boolean.** Memory, relation, disposition, standpoint/value, lineage, reconstruction policy, and model/runtime continuity may vary independently; Authority is separate again.
8. **Historical `MEMORY != CURRENT STATE`.** Later live work broadens Memory/Status, but this earlier analytical firewall remains a genuine source position and an important falsifier of two-bucket collapse.
9. **One adaptive Persona may outperform a multi-Persona topology.** Plurality/fission is not automatically useful; multiple branches can add fragmentation, coordination cost, and authority confusion.
10. **Long-term Memory may be more dangerous than helpful.** Durability enlarges poisoning, coercion, privacy, dossier, capture, and ghost-influence surfaces.
11. **Local sovereignty may be illusory.** User-held bytes do not guarantee cognitive sovereignty if high-quality reasoning, framing, hidden personalization, or context compilation remains centralized.
12. **Audit plus governance may create false security.** Same-root auditors and review layers can add confidence without independent error discovery; Control must remain safe when audit fails.
13. **PCS-SHAI may reproduce cognitive concentration.** A portability/sovereignty protocol can itself become centralization, monoculture, or protocol capture.
14. **Merge-as-reunification is weakened.** Diverged branches may have no coherent single identity to restore; successor C is a live alternative.
15. **Provider compatibility is not established.** “Provider replacement must be possible” is an intent; behaviorally compatible, expertise portable, relationship continuous, and fully independent remain unproven.
16. **Persisted Self anchor is unnecessary.** The explicit correction retains runtime receiver/reference and derived or hybrid self-model alternatives.
17. **Persisted Current Status is unnecessary.** Minimal, rich, derived, and hybrid candidates remain unresolved.
18. **Deletion can be normal.** “Deletion is unnatural” was explicitly withdrawn; research-history preservation cannot be generalized into Persona-memory immutability.
19. **Long use need not imply capture.** Relationship duration and dependency are empirically separable; an intimacy-only suspicion can itself misclassify legitimate value.
20. **Human or external evaluation is not ground truth.** Persona self-description may be wrong, but an external evaluator can also be wrong; triangulation is required.
