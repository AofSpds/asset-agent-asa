# Manual QA of 86 v0.2 relation candidates

Every new relation was reread endpoint-to-endpoint after generation. Twelve mechanically plausible links were materially revised:

- four evidence-like verbs were reduced to `REFINES` or `CONSTRAINS` so Codex inference and same-root agreement do not masquerade as independent strengthening;
- three indirect or wrong hypothesis endpoints were replaced with the actual source/live target;
- deletion undecidability was changed from `WEAKENS` to `REFINES`;
- de facto Authority was changed from weakening the formal firewall to constraining its practical adequacy;
- perceived-realism and external-reconstruction relations were changed from opposition to refinement;
- the slow-poison and typed-unknown experiments now test their exact failure modes.

Final disposition: 74 accurate candidates and 12 corrected candidates. All 172 resulting endpoints resolve. These remain unvalidated Codex relation candidates, not Owner-tagged relations.
