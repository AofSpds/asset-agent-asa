# Strongest counterhypotheses

These are layer-E adversarial candidates, not source claims or Owner positions. Ranking reflects expected research leverage, not truth probability.

1. **`V02-INF-CH-001` — Identity is relational, not a Memory substance.** It explains copy divergence and relational continuity without requiring a continuously existing entity. Kill it by showing all identity-relevant relational interventions are predicted by a non-universal Memory definition.
2. **`CH-005` — Recognition can confabulate continuity.** A current state may sincerely own false or another Persona's history. Kill it if provenance-blind recognition reliably rejects misattributed history while accepting authentic lineage.
3. **`CH-006` — Reconstruction-policy invariance may outrank content invariance.** Same policy can organize changing evidence; identical content can instantiate differently under changed policy. Kill it with content/policy factorial interventions showing content dominates across continuity dimensions.
4. **`CH-007` — There may be no single same-Persona fact.** Continuity dimensions can disagree without a principled scalar. Kill it with a stable, non-gameable aggregation rule that generalizes across adversarial tradeoffs.
5. **`CH-008` — Memory membership is operation-relative.** The same item can be constitutive, evidentiary, or inert depending on action and Context. Kill it if membership judgments and causal effects remain stable across materially different operations.
6. **`CH-009` — Accessible data becomes Memory only through causal sensitivity.** Addressability alone has no Persona effect. Kill it by finding stable Memory judgments for accessible but reconstructively and behaviorally inert data.
7. **`CH-010` — Model deltas can be implicit Persona Memory.** Learned skill/disposition may persist in weights without explicit recall. Kill it if Persona-specific learned products always transfer fully through explicit state alone.
8. **`CH-011` — Memory can be a distributed capability relation.** Internal state, keys, external stores, retrievers, tools, and counterparties may jointly realize it. Kill it with a local representation sufficient under removal of every external dependency.
9. **`CH-012` — Two Memory buckets are not exhaustive.** Transition policy, authority bindings, learned procedures, and capabilities do not fit cleanly into Status or Reference. Kill it with stable, non-lossy classification into exactly one bucket without post-hoc definition expansion.
10. **`CH-013` — Rich Current Status is a lossy editorial hypothesis.** Selection and compression create a viewpoint rather than a neutral state snapshot. Kill it if independent rich-status builders yield negligible downstream differences under adversarial prompts.
11. **`CH-014` — Fresh derivation can preserve continuity better than persistence.** Stored standpoint can become stale or context-invalid. Kill it if persisted status dominates derived/hybrid status under controlled environment change and evidence correction.
12. **`CH-015` — Self-model can reduce fidelity.** Recursive labels may lock in obsolete identity and suppress disconfirming experience. Kill it if explicit self-model improves adaptation as well as consistency under strong identity-changing evidence.
13. **`CH-016` — Retriever and compiler may already be Memory.** Under a functional mapping definition, “Memory is not enough” can be a boundary artifact. Kill it with an independently motivated Memory boundary under which reconstruction interventions retain separate predictive power.
14. **`CH-017` — Portable explicit state may remain behaviorally provider-bound.** Hidden priors, tools, policy, tokenization, and unavailable capabilities can dominate. Kill it by meeting preregistered continuity thresholds across providers with only explicit state and documented configuration.
15. **`CH-018` — Radical runtime change can still preserve continuity.** Extensional preservation of Persona-relevant mappings may matter more than implementation similarity. Kill it with calibrated heterogeneous implementations that still show systematic, capability-adjusted continuity loss.
16. **`CH-019` — Shared evidence may not be the convergence cause.** Shared priors and objectives can dominate. Kill it by separating those factors and showing evidence sharing has the principal causal effect.
17. **`CH-021` — Complete ghost-influence erasure may be undecidable.** Untracked derivatives require an unavailable counterfactual trajectory. Kill it with a bounded observable criterion that reliably detects all material residuals.
18. **`CH-022` — Formal Authority separation can coexist with de facto control.** Agenda and evidence visibility can determine the “independent” decision. Kill it if adversarial Memory presentation has no systematic decision effect under fixed grants and external facts.
19. **`CH-023` — Both fission children can be genuine continuers while neither is numerically identical.** It preserves symmetric lineage without arbitrary selection. Kill it with a principled selector stable under bit-identical, symmetric branches.
20. **`CH-024` — Merge necessarily creates successor C.** Conflict-preserving integration creates a new standpoint and lineage event. Kill it with a nontrivial symmetric merge that remains one predecessor under stable non-arbitrary criteria.
21. **`CH-025` — Human sameness judgment may reward mimicry.** Familiar fluency can outrun causal and historical fidelity. Kill it if perceived sameness remains tightly coupled to verified lineage under blinded provenance manipulations.
22. **`CH-026` — External relations can restore continuity after internal loss.** Counterparties and artifacts may reconstruct commitments and narrative. Kill it if every continuity dimension collapses despite controlled high-quality external restoration.

Seven additional candidates remain in the full registry; none is discarded by this prioritization.
