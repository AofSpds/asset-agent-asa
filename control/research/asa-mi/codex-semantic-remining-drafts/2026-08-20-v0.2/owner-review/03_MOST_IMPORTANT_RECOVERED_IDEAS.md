# Most important recovered ideas

1. **Atomicity has a stopping rule.** Split claims only when doing so improves semantic clarity; preserve compound narrative when decomposition destroys context or meaning (`V02-LIVE-NEW-0001`).
2. **Prior art has no adoption effect by existence.** A found reference does not imply correctness, endorsement, semantic equivalence, design authority, adoption, or an ASA-MI requirement (`V02-LIVE-NEW-0002`).
3. **Research-history lifecycle is not Persona-memory lifecycle.** Preservation rules for ASA-MI artifacts must not be generalized to all Persona data or deletion semantics (`V02-LIVE-NEW-0003`).
4. **Status dimensions and Status representations are independent questions.** A dimension can be persisted, derived, runtime-bound, transient, reference-only, or unnecessary under any of the minimal/rich/derived/hybrid families (`V02-LIVE-NEW-0004`).
5. **UNKNOWN is not zero.** Quantification intent cannot invent numerical certainty or convert missing classification into a scalar value (`V02-LIVE-NEW-0005`).
6. **UNOBSERVED is not ABSENT.** Observation state and existence/value state require separate representation (`V02-LIVE-NEW-0006`).
