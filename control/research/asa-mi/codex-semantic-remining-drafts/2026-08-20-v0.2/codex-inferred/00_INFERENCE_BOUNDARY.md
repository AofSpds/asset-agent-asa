# v0.2 Codex-inference boundary

Everything in this directory is layer E: a Codex-generated research candidate produced **after** source-faithful mining and QA. It is not a recovered source claim, Owner position, normative AAA requirement, validation result, architecture decision, or production authorization.

Candidates are intentionally plural and conflicting. Their purpose is to expand the thought-space and expose discriminating tests. Count targets are applied only after semantic deduplication; a paraphrase does not become a second candidate.

`CURRENT_HYPOTHESIS != FINAL_TRUTH`, `CODEX_INFERENCE != OWNER_POSITION`, and `AGREEMENT_COUNT != INDEPENDENT_EVIDENCE` apply to every record here.
