## 1. Foundational worldview

### W-01 — Reality precedes the current model

No current proposition, model, theory, interpretation, or hypothesis is assumed to be an immutable final truth.

The project operates from the position that reality may contradict the current model, and when that happens the model must remain revisable.

The practical interpretation of the Owner's statement `불변한 것은 없다` is therefore:

> **Do not pre-commit any current interpretation as permanently immutable or finally true.**

This formulation intentionally avoids turning `nothing is immutable` itself into an untouchable absolute proposition.

### W-02 — No final conclusion is required for current action

Because current interpretations remain revisable, the project distinguishes:

- `FINAL_TRUTH`
- `CURRENT_BEST_HYPOTHESIS`
- `CURRENT_OPERATIONAL_DECISION`

The project does make decisions and establish working hypotheses. It does not need to claim that those decisions or hypotheses are final truth.

At a given time, the project may act on the hypothesis that is currently judged closest to reality while preserving its replaceability.

### W-03 — Hypotheses accumulate, compete, strengthen, weaken, and can be replaced

The project should continuously accumulate hypotheses and evidence.

A currently established hypothesis may:

- become stronger through new evidence;
- become narrower through counterexamples;
- coexist with an alternative hypothesis;
- be replaced by a better explanatory hypothesis;
- remain unresolved when evidence is insufficient.

Replacement does not erase history. Earlier hypotheses and the evidence available at the time remain research history.

### W-04 — Research progress means moving toward better explanations, not defending prior beliefs

The objective is not to preserve an existing thesis.

The objective is to move toward hypotheses that better explain observed reality and survive stronger falsification.

Therefore disagreement, counterexamples, and alternative models are research assets rather than process failures.

## 2. Current established Persona–Memory hypothesis

### Hypothesis state

- `HYPOTHESIS_ID`: `ASA-MI-H-IDENTITY-MEMORY-v0.1`
- `STATE`: `ESTABLISHED_WORKING_HYPOTHESIS`
- `CONFIRMATION_STATE`: `UNCONFIRMED`
- `INIT_ROLE`: `INIT_BASIS`
- `REPLACEABILITY`: `REPLACEABLE`
- `METAPHYSICAL_TRUTH_CLAIM`: `NONE`

### H-01 — Continuous substance is not assumed

The project does **not** begin by assuming that a Persona is an immutable entity or a continuously flowing process that must persist without interruption through time.

The current hypothesis is closer to the view that what exists operationally at any moment is a **current state**, potentially composed of a bundle of instantiated processes.

The existence of a single, continuously persisting metaphysical entity is neither required nor confirmed.

### H-02 — Conscious/Persona continuity may be a current-state construction

The current hypothesis allows that the sense of continuity may be constructed from the current state rather than proving the existence of an actually continuous substance.

A current state can recognize prior events, relationships, experiences, interpretations, and earlier states as `its own history` through memory.

The resulting phenomenon can appear and operate as temporal identity/continuity even if the underlying process itself was interrupted.

This is `UNCONFIRMED`.

### H-03 — `Identity = Memory` is a strong research hypothesis, not an established fact

For this workstream, `Identity = Memory` is retained as a deliberately strong hypothesis and falsification target.

It does **not** mean:

> A continuously existing Identity substance owns a continuous Memory and is therefore proven to be the same entity.

The current intended interpretation is closer to:

> **A presently instantiated state may construct identity/continuity by recognizing a remembered past as its own history. Memory may therefore be a primary substrate through which identity-like continuity is instantiated.**

Whether Memory is necessary, sufficient, primary, or only one component remains open to operational evidence.

### H-04 — Process continuity is not an INIT prerequisite

The target Persona is a digital object expected to operate in a world where instantiation can be discontinuous.

Normal lifecycle conditions may include:

- process termination;
- restart;
- reconstruction;
- device migration;
- model replacement;
- provider replacement;
- local/cloud transition;
- clone/fission;
- future successor creation.

Therefore the INIT architecture should not require uninterrupted process continuity as a prerequisite for Persona existence.

The research question becomes:

> **Can a newly instantiated current state appropriately recognize and use its prior history such that operational Persona continuity is reconstructed despite process discontinuity?**

### H-05 — Digital discontinuity is a native condition, not an exception

The project is not designing an entity that is assumed to remain continuously instantiated inside a continuously running cognitive process.

It is designing an entity intended to be repeatedly instantiated in a digital environment where discontinuity is a normal condition.

This makes reconstruction, portability, lineage, and state interpretation first-class research problems rather than edge cases.

## 3. Open boundary question — What counts as Memory?

`MEMORY` is intentionally **not yet fully defined**.

This is now an explicit research question rather than an implicit assumption.

Candidate categories to examine separately include:

- episodic memory;
- semantic/personal knowledge;
- procedural expertise;
- relationship memory;
- preferences and heuristics derived from experience;
- behavioral/dispositional state;
- current interpretation/standpoint;
- self-model;
- retrieval/reconstruction policy;
- model/runtime prior.

The project must avoid both failure modes:

1. **Definition too narrow** — important identity-bearing state is excluded from Memory.
2. **Definition too broad** — every Persona-bearing component is relabeled as Memory, making `Identity = Memory` unfalsifiable.

The boundary of Memory therefore remains a deliberate subject of adversarial research.

## 4. Discussion and challenge rule

This baseline exists to prevent repeated zero-base reconstruction of the Owner's worldview.

Future researchers, planners, RED teams, architects, and successors should **not** treat the baseline as unquestionable.

They should instead begin from the recorded baseline and identify what they are adding or challenging.

Useful challenge classes include:

- `NEW_EVIDENCE`
- `NEW_COUNTEREXAMPLE`
- `NEW_INTERPRETATION`
- `ALTERNATIVE_HYPOTHESIS`
- `EXPERIMENTAL_RESULT`
- `SCOPE_REFINEMENT`

A challenge should state which existing hypothesis it affects and whether the proposed result is:

- strengthen;
- weaken;
- narrow;
- coexist;
- replace;
- remain unresolved.

Agreement count is not evidence weight. Correlated reasoning/common cognitive roots remain a standing concern.
