## 1. Why this research exists

The research begins from a question:

Can we create a computational substrate capable of supporting a human-compatible Persona?

The problem is that common implementation abstractions may be insufficient:

- Persona = persistent object
- Memory = stored records
- Identity = identifier
- Relation = connection
- Change = state overwrite

These may be useful engineering choices, but they must not automatically become assumptions about reality or human continuity.

---

## 2. Research purpose

The goal is not to copy humans literally.

The goal is to discover abstractions capable of representing important aspects of human-compatible continuity:

- memory
- identity
- continuity
- succession
- relation
- change
- history
- perspective
- context
- uncertainty
- interpretation
- individuation
- sovereignty

Research direction:

Reality / Human Experience
→ Philosophical / Scientific / Mathematical / Computational Abstraction
→ World Model
→ Computational Contact
→ Persona Substrate

## 4. Core questions

Identity:

Does memory define identity?

Continuity:

What remains continuous through change?

Succession:

How are successors related to predecessors?

Relation:

Is relation merely a connection, or can it be constitutive?

History:

How should past meaning and current interpretation coexist?

Change:

Is change only state mutation, or a deeper generative process?

---

## 5. P0 interpretation

P0 does not mean nothing can be known.

It means current meaning should not be treated as an eternal immutable essence.

Important balance:

Change possibility
+
Historical integrity
+
Successor possibility

Past records may be preserved while future interpretations evolve.

---

## 6. World Model minimum criterion

Primary question:

CAN_THE_MODEL_FAITHFULLY_CONTAIN_AND_OPERATIONALIZE_ITS_OWN_WORLDVIEW?

A model must not only describe a worldview.

The worldview must influence the actual model structure and produce consequences that can be examined.

However, current ASA-MI concepts are not universal mandatory primitives.

A different worldview is acceptable if it faithfully embodies itself and serves the purpose.
