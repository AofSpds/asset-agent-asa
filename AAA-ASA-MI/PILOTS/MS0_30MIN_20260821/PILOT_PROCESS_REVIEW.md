# MS0 30-Minute Pilot — Process Review

STATE = PROCESS_OBSERVATION / NON_NORMATIVE / NO_MODEL_SELECTION_AUTHORITY

## WHAT_WORKED

- The designated context set was sufficient to recover naming guardrails, P0 revision posture, major hypothesis families, the tournament design, and the distinction between pilot evidence and MS0 authority.
- Required candidate fields forced each seed to expose its primitive or equivalent, demoted concepts, assumptions, and failure surface.
- The materially-distinct test produced eight computational families rather than eight renamed graphs.
- A single pressure matrix made recurring hidden commitments visible across families.
- Independent positive and negative findings preserved high-upside candidates without erasing their risks and preserved conservative candidates without declaring them uninteresting.
- Routing vocabulary avoided validation-style PASS/FAIL claims.

## WHAT_WAS_TOO_SHALLOW

- No candidate received a formal semantics, executable spike, complexity bound, or adversarial counterexample set.
- Change/history was judged from plausible constructions, not demonstrated reconstruction.
- Human-familiar projection, sovereignty, individuation, liveness, and membrane hypotheses were not exercised in shared scenarios.
- The distinctness claim is family-level; it does not prove that these eight are the best or widest MS0 field.
- External prior-art attribution and terminology were not source-verified.

## WHERE_CONTEXT_WAS_MISSING

- No canonical pilot toy worlds or common query pack were present.
- No explicit ranking of which current hypotheses must be preserved versus merely tested.
- No boundedness budget (runtime, state count, code size, or engineering time) was defined.
- No evidence standard specified how a lightweight observation becomes enough to route a candidate.
- The designated records summarize earlier source provenance; not every underlying Owner statement was independently checked.

## WHERE_CANDIDATES_BECAME_TOO_SIMILAR

- C01, C04, and C05 all resist closure, but through different semantics: derivability/conflict, admissible solutions, and local-to-global compatibility. Full MS0 must test whether those differences survive common scenarios.
- C02 and C03 both represent change, but C02 privileges recorded past occurrences while C03 privileges possible/observable future behavior.
- C05 and C08 share abstract compositional mathematics, yet one centers contextual restriction/gluing and the other transformation composition. C08 remained NOT_PROVEN because its bare form supplied too little world semantics.

## WHERE_TIME_WAS_WASTED

- The local `main` ref was stale and lacked every designated context file. Safe remote inspection and fast-forward preflight consumed part of the context budget, though it prevented work on the wrong repository state.
- The large succession checkpoint is valuable but duplicates many earlier findings; a compact current-state manifest with provenance pointers would reduce reconstruction time.
- Matrix marks and prose partly duplicate each other; Full MS0 should generate detailed cards from a structured source to avoid manual repetition.

## WHERE_TIMEBOX_HELPED

- It prevented deep attachment to the first plausible candidate.
- It forced OPEN issues to be recorded rather than resolved rhetorically.
- It exposed that candidate enumeration is much cheaper than fair comparison.
- It made closure artifacts, Git state, and process failures first-class outputs.

## WHETHER_8_CANDIDATES_WAS_REALISTIC

YES_FOR_SEED_DIVERGENCE / NO_FOR_SERIOUS_EVALUATION_IN_30_MINUTES.

Eight materially different family seeds were realistic because the task permitted known formal/CS families and short cards. Eight formally specified, source-grounded, executable candidates under a common gate are not realistic in the same budget. The Full MS0 phrase “serious candidate” should require an explicit semantic core and at least one discriminating example before Main Round entry.

## WHETHER_POSITIVE_NEGATIVE_FILTERS_WERE_USEFUL

YES. They prevented two common collapses:

1. C05's unusually high contextual upside did not erase its topology/complexity risk.
2. C04's low-commitment robustness did not erase its combinatorial and constraint-language lock-in.

The filters should remain independent, but Full MS0 should require evidence references per finding rather than impression-only prose.

## WHAT_TO_CHANGE_BEFORE_FULL_MS0

1. Add a compact, provenance-tagged `CURRENT_RESEARCH_STATE` manifest so context reconstruction does not depend on one long checkpoint.
2. Define 3–4 implementation-neutral toy worlds before candidate elaboration: historical reinterpretation without rewrite; unresolved conflict; structural fission/merge; context/scale change.
3. Define a common query pack and explicit candidate right to reject/reframe a query.
4. Require each candidate to state a minimal semantic kernel, executable representation, and at least one predeclared abandonment observation.
5. Run distinctness twice: once before elaboration and again after common scenarios, merging candidates whose operational behavior converges.
6. Preserve candidate-family breadth but allow replacement after a short feasibility screen; do not invest equally in a clearly NOT_PROVEN seed.
7. Capture pressure results in a structured ledger and render prose/matrices from it.
8. Add a separate epistemic-model-versus-world-model check; C01 and C06 show how easily those layers can be conflated.
9. Add ontology-relocation inspection: identify where each candidate hides commitments even when it claims to avoid primitives.
10. Keep pilot rankings quarantined; Full MS0 should regenerate candidate IDs/order and compare without exposing pilot representatives until its first independent pass is complete.

## RECOMMENDED_FULL_MS0_TIME_BUDGET_UPDATE

RECOMMENDATION = 12–16 focused hours across staged checkpoints, not one uninterrupted run.

- 1–2 hours: provenance-tagged context reconstruction and common toy/query/falsification contract.
- 3–4 hours: eight candidate semantic kernels plus distinctness review.
- 4–5 hours: common executable or worked pressure tests and independent positive/negative evidence.
- 2–3 hours: representative/finalist deepening and cross-candidate attack.
- 1–2 hours: process review, meeting memory, eliminated-value register, and Git closure.

EARLY_STOP_RULE = If post-scenario distinctness collapses or a shared blocker appears, stop ranking and return to model-space design rather than consuming the remaining budget defending the field.

BIGGEST_PROCESS_FAILURE = The pilot could route seeds only from reasoned plausibility because a shared executable scenario/query contract was not available; the resulting representatives are process demonstrations, not comparative model evidence.
