## Was the evidence sufficient?

The evidence was sufficient for commit-pinned routing because each target supplied all A1–A12 headings, a bounded probe, query contact, an assumption table, a successor path, and a falsification proposal. It was not sufficient for independent validation, owner acceptance, or final selection. Three targets could be routed non-admission directly from exact-target gaps: P01's trace is not derivable, P03 does not yet escape interface/epistemic status, and P04's temporal composition is under-specified. P02 had enough internal evidence for serious-candidate admission, but its declared abandonment tests remain future candidate work.

## Did gate compliance become semantic thinness?

Yes, selectively. The uniform A1–A12 structure made omissions easy to locate, but fluent compliance also created an initial impression of equal maturity. P01, P03, and P04 each name the correct risks and revision mechanics while leaving the central discriminator partly outside the artifact: a native world semantics, a reproducible continuation procedure, or a coherent temporal compatibility rule. Headings and self-critique did not substitute for derivation. P02 was less thin because its small marking example produced several consequences from the kernel rather than merely describing them.

## Were four proposals realistic?

Four proposals were realistic for a breadth-first admission pilot and gave useful family contrast. Four fully qualified candidates in one short authoring pass were not realistic. Independent V1–V12 review exposed operational defects and ontology gaps that require focused successor work. A more reliable cadence is four initial dossiers, one or two routed serious candidates, and explicit successor rounds for the remainder.

## Estimated cost of eight qualified candidates

ESTIMATED_8_QUALIFIED_CANDIDATE_COST = 10–14 focused agent-hours, excluding implementation beyond bounded probes.

Basis:

- 6–8 hours for eight semantically distinct authoring dossiers with internally replayed probes and explicit commitment/assumption audits;
- 3–4 hours for independent commit-pinned V1–V12 paired admission review, identity verification, and receipts;
- 1–2 hours for likely successor repair, re-freezing, and closure coordination.

This estimate assumes the eight candidates are already selected as promising families. Generating a larger pool to obtain eight admissions, or executing full empirical abandonment experiments, would cost materially more. The pilot suggests that doubling proposal count without doubling semantic probe time would mostly double polished dossiers, not qualified candidates.

## Process recommendation

Retain the exact-target and no-averaging rules. Before freezing the next batch, require authors to mechanically replay every micro-probe and add a one-sentence statement of the native semantic consequence that remains when the domain payload/schema is replaced. That preflight directly targets the two most common failures without turning the validator into a proposal repairer.
