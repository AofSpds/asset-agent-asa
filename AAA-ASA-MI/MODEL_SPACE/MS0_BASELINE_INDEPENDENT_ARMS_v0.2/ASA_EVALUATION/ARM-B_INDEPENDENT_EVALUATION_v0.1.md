## Evidence reviewed

Blind-divergence ledger, nine deep proposals, dependency-free semantic probes, mechanical preflight/pressure tests, diversity rescue/saturation audit, local finalist decision, and RETURN PACKET.

## Positive evaluation

1. **ARM-B has the clearest ontogenesis discriminator.** The hostile comparison between supplied correspondence (CCP), generative transformation (TRCC), and inferred similarity (PTL) is especially valuable. A one-to-two before/after pattern is not automatically fission: only TRCC derives it when an explicit split rule exists.
2. **TRCC produces native structural change rather than merely recording it.** Fresh creation, predecessor non-persistence, conflict/concurrency, causal partial order, exact replay, and zero/one/many lineage are strong World-Model-level consequences.
3. **CCP remains conservative where TRCC is aggressive.** It can preserve UNKNOWN/UNDEFINED/DISPUTED and failed globalization rather than inventing one world or one lineage.
4. **Continuous counterpressure was not suppressed.** VTD is retained precisely because smooth bounded change reveals the arbitrariness of pure event slicing.
5. **Anti-convergence search was meaningful.** Anonymous measure-transfer dynamics and observable/symmetry bases showed that useful semantics need not begin with local graph-like individuals.
6. **The finalist distinction is strong.** D2 TRCC generates a split D1 CCP cannot infer; D1 CCP generates a gluing obstruction D2 must reinterpret.

## Negative evaluation / unresolved risk

1. **Both finalist kernels have high-impact selection ontology.** TRCC depends on type/match/L-K-R granularity; CCP depends on context/cover/overlap equality. Small representational choices can reverse conclusions.
2. **TRCC is not sufficient for continuous becoming.** Equally plausible discretizations can alter causal claims. This is a characteristic, not peripheral, failure.
3. **CCP is weak on native causal transformation.** When every important change needs an external bridge or rewrite law, another kernel may be the actual dynamics engine.
4. **The likely combined architecture risks primitive accumulation.** A CCP↔TRCC hybrid is scientifically interesting only if each yields nonredundant consequences and the combined commitment surface stays controlled.
5. **Qualified local saturation is still local.** It is not proof that the formal search space or family priors are exhausted.
