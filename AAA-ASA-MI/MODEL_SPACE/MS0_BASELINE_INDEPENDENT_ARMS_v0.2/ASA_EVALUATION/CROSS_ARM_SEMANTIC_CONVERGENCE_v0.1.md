## 2. The most important convergence

The baseline does **not** converge on a single named model. It converges on a probable separation of semantic jobs:

A. `CONTEXTUAL_CONSTITUTION / NON_GLOBAL_COMPATIBILITY`
B. `GENERATIVE_CHANGE / CAUSAL_REPLAY`
C. `CONTINUOUS_CHANGE` as a compulsory adversary or companion
D. `INDIVIDUATION / CLOSURE / GENEALOGY` as a derived or optional analysis layer
E. `INTERFACE_COMPOSITION` and `PREDICTIVE_INFERENCE` as important but non-foundational specializations unless future evidence changes this.

This is a stronger result than vocabulary convergence because each ARM obtained materially similar failure surfaces and proposed similar next experiments.

HYBRID_ATTRACTOR = STRONG

## 5. Interpretation of convergence

CONVERGENCE != TRUTH.

The present experiment cannot isolate the source of convergence because all ARMs shared:
- the same substantive execution packet and pressure rubric;
- the same project objective;
- likely related foundation-model priors and formal training;
- similar constraints around history, non-closure, identity, and global time.

Current causal attribution:
- PROBLEM_INDUCED_CONVERGENCE = PLAUSIBLE_HIGH
- RUBRIC_INDUCED_CONVERGENCE = PLAUSIBLE_HIGH
- MODEL_PRIOR_INDUCED_CONVERGENCE = PLAUSIBLE_MEDIUM_HIGH
- CONTEXT_INDUCED_CONVERGENCE = HIGH

The experiment demonstrates independent execution-path convergence under shared conditions, not independent epistemic origin in the strongest sense.

## 6. ASA decision on next research design

LONG_RUN_v0.3_SHOULD_RUN_UNCHANGED = NO

Reason: the baseline already found a strong attractor. More identical long runs risk spending compute repeatedly rediscovering the same F1/F2 decomposition.

Recommended successor design:
1. Preserve this baseline unchanged as Batch-0 evidence.
2. Create new anti-attractor ARMs with asymmetric exclusions or priors.
3. At least one ARM should forbid contextual patch/gluing models as a starting basis.
4. At least one ARM should forbid local rewrite/event models as a starting basis.
5. At least one ARM should begin continuous/measure/transformation-first and attempt to subsume both F1/F2 without importing them as opaque payload.
6. Require a direct falsification target: produce a model that beats the two-job decomposition on native consequence, commitment surface, and 별 feasibility.
7. Only after this anti-attractor batch should the D1-within-D3 / CCP↔TRCC hybrid be subjected to formal paired model-design validation.
