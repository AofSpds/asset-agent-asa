## Evidence reviewed

ARM-A protocol, blind divergence map, nine deep proposals, deterministic micro-probes and replay receipt, preflight/pressure tests, diversity rescue/routing/finalist decision, and RETURN PACKET.

## Positive evaluation

1. **Search breadth is genuinely material rather than nominal.** 37 ideas span constraint/admissibility, occurrence/rewrite, open composition, lineage, contextual locality, continuous dynamics, generative semantics, and explicit diversity-rescue bases.
2. **Anti-convergence search actually changed the basis.** The late Invariant Transformation World (ITW) did not simply rename the leading patch/constraint family. It produced orbit/invariant semantics and a distinct irreversibility/carrier-change failure surface.
3. **Mechanical contact is good for a baseline research ARM.** Nine deterministic probes gave executable consequences: entailment, concurrency, deadlock, local/global obstruction, fission/merge, bifurcation, prediction, semantic currentization, and invariance.
4. **The negative filter is substantive.** The ARM repeatedly exposed ontology relocation into variable/type choice, cover choice, ports, observation vocabulary, coordinates, detector, and transformation family rather than claiming ontology freedom.
5. **Finalist pair is operationally distinct.** D4 LPCW's local-to-global obstruction is not the same computation as D1 AHCK's admissible-history entailment/inconsistency.

## Negative evaluation / unresolved risk

1. **Saturation is explicitly not proven.** D9 arrived late and remained less pressure-tested than D1/D4; this is evidence that further basis changes can still matter.
2. **D4 LPCW's strongest feature is also its largest liability.** A cover and overlap/equality regime can manufacture the very locality/globality distinction being measured. Cover-refinement invariance is therefore a blocking scientific question before promotion.
3. **D1 AHCK can collapse into a theory-state.** If realistic consequences are merely encoded directly into constraints, it stops being a constitutive World Model and becomes an expressive admissibility/epistemic layer.
4. **Continuous change remains an external pressure.** D6 CPA showed that discrete/rewrite-like accounts cannot be assumed sufficient. D1 can host differential constraints only by expanding its commitment surface.
5. **Model-space selection remains qualitative.** The pressure tests are strong conceptual tests but not independent formal proofs, empirical adequacy tests, or complexity measurements.
