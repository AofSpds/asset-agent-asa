## Evidence reviewed

Research ledger covering 36 blind-divergence ideas, eight deep proposals, deterministic probe code/replay, 17 pressure families, diversity rescue, local routing, finalist signatures, formal-validation suggestions, plus ARM-C RETURN PACKET.

## Positive evaluation

1. **ARM-C has the most explicit pressure-test matrix.** It consistently records SURVIVES/DAMAGED/BREAKS outcomes and ties them to observable failure modes rather than generic claims of support.
2. **D3 CCRA has unusually strong non-closure semantics.** It distinguishes local satisfiability, absence of a global gluing, multiple repaired gluings, UNKNOWN, UNDEFINED, and DISPUTED without forcing one actuality.
3. **D1 WLRF has a compact executable change kernel.** Guard entailment, conservation, replay, structural succession, conflict, and many-to-many lineage are strong local consequences with a relatively small core.
4. **The ARM explicitly rejects ontology-free rhetoric.** Every candidate's ontology relocation is named and pressure-tested.
5. **Continuous change, interaction, prediction, closure, and behavioral equivalence remain visible as separate semantic jobs.** The ARM does not let the two finalists erase these surviving counterbases.
6. **The proposed D1-within-D3 test is scientifically well targeted.** It asks whether local rewrite and global obstruction remain nonredundant rather than assuming a hybrid is better.

## Negative evaluation / unresolved risk

1. **D3 CCRA is expensive semantically and computationally.** Cover, overlap, equality, constraint fragment, solver closure, patch semantics, and gluing are all consequential commitments.
2. **D1 WLRF breaks on irreducibly continuous change and lacks native perspective/context.** These are not minor missing features given the project objectives.
3. **Local saturation is supported but not proven.** The failure to find a ninth strong proposal after rescue may reflect genuine saturation or a shared formal/model prior.
4. **D3's strongest result can be engineered by the cover.** Adversarial cover refinement is therefore a mandatory test before any model admission.
5. **D1 branch currentization remains under-specified.** Selecting one live branch risks silently importing authority/actuality semantics that the model otherwise avoids.
