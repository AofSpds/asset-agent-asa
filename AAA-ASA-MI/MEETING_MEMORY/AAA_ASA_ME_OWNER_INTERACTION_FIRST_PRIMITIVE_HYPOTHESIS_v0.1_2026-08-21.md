## Core clarification
nemo clarified that the current hypothesis is not merely that directed mapping/function is primitive. The stronger working hypothesis is:

- Interaction may be prior to stable participants A/B.
- Directed source→target may be a time/causal/observational projection of a deeper interaction/coupling.
- Bidirectional or multi-directional interaction may be closer to the primitive than a single directed arrow.
- Source/target states may be transient time-local observations or cognitive reifications rather than persistent objects.
- Observation itself is an interaction/mapping inside the model, not an external meta-operation.

## Current candidate hierarchy
1. Directed mapping primitive
2. Multi-directional mapping bundle
3. Joint coupling / interaction primitive (currently strongest Owner hypothesis)

## Important open questions
- Are participants/boundaries emergent from interaction?
- Is directionality primitive or projected from interaction under time/causal perspective?
- What minimal structure is needed for composition without smuggling in persistent objects?
- Can the same substrate natively express Persona continuity, differentiation, merge, re-differentiation, protocol bundles, and Human–ASA bundle formation?

## Constraint
Do not convert this hypothesis into a fixed design requirement yet. It is a candidate to be attacked by independent committee models and compared against object-first, typed-morphism, objectless-composition, and other alternatives.
