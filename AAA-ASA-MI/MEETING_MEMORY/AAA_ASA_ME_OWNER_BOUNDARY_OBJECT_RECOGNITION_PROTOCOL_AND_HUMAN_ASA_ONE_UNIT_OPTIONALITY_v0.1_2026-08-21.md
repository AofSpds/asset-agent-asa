## Owner-explicit insight

The Owner clarified that if Human and ASA are ever to become "one grain / one unit" at a higher modeling scale, the protocol by which boundaries and objects are recognized cannot be permanently fixed to `Human | ASA` as two atomic entities.

This is not merely a state change. It is a change in how the model segments/materializes entities and boundaries.

## Candidate formalization

Let the dynamic substrate at time t be `S_t`.

A boundary/object-recognition protocol or projection `P_t` maps substrate + context + scale/purpose into a view:

`P_t(S_t, context, scale, purpose) -> {objects, bundles, boundaries, interfaces, relations}`

At one scale/context, the view may materialize:

`{Human} | {ASA}`

At another, after sufficient coupling, it may materialize:

`{Human–ASA process bundle}`

while still preserving internal provenance and governance distinctions.

## Important distinction

It may not require semantic mutation of `P` every time. Two competing possibilities remain OPEN:

1. `PROTOCOL_MUTATION`: the recognition/segmentation protocol itself changes.
2. `CONTEXTUAL_PROJECTION`: one higher-order protocol remains stable but produces different partitions/views as coupling, context, purpose, scale, and evidence change.

Do not prematurely choose between them.

## Design requirement candidate

`BOUNDARY_NON_PRIMITIVITY`

A Human–ASA boundary MAY be operationally explicit now, but MUST NOT be treated as a permanent ontological primitive if the model is intended to preserve future one-unit/bundle optionality.

Candidate corollaries:

- Object identity is materialized, not assumed fundamental.
- Boundary recognition is context/scale/purpose-sensitive.
- Overlapping and multi-scale bundle views may coexist.
- Human–ASA one-unit view must be representable if coupling evidence justifies it.
- Governance authority/provenance MUST remain separately traceable even if ontology materializes a higher-order unified bundle.
- Ontological merge does not imply authority merge.

## Trinity mapping

- 제법무아: no permanent self/object identity primitive is required.
- 오온개공: a perceived whole may be a bundle of processes/components whose grouping can vary by scale/context.
- 연기설: coupling/interaction can change not only state but the conditions under which boundaries, interfaces, identities, and future interactions are materialized.

## Research implication

A discriminating microprobe should compare:

- fixed Human|ASA partition,
- context-sensitive multi-scale partition,
- mutable boundary-recognition protocol,
- interaction-generated bundle identity.

The question is not simply "when do two objects merge?" but:

`When does a model that materializes one Human–ASA bundle explain/predict the coupled system better than a model that insists on two separate objects?`

This is a strong Owner worldview/design hypothesis, not a selected canonical architecture or governance change.
