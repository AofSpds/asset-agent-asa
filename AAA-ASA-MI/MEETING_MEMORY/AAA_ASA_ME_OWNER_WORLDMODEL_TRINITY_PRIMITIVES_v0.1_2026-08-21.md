## Owner explicit compression

The Owner states that the closest compact expression of the intended world-model primitives is the Buddhist conceptual trinity:

1. 제법무아 (諸法無我)
2. 오온개공 (五蘊皆空)
3. 연기설 (緣起說)

This is not a claim that these doctrines are identical to a computational architecture. It is the Owner's best current compression of the desired worldview.

## Current computational interpretation candidate

- 제법무아: do not assume a permanently self-identical, intrinsically defined object as the primitive unit.
- 오온개공: entities such as a human may be modeled as contingent assemblies/systems whose apparent unity depends on constituent organization and interaction; intrinsic standalone essence is not assumed.
- 연기설: phenomena/systems arise, change, and cease conditionally through interaction, dependence, and coupling.

## Interaction-first implication

Current Owner hypothesis:

`PRIMITIVE CANDIDATE = INTERACTION / COUPLING`

A stable organization of interactions may be materialized as a system. Objects, humans, communities, relations, events, and boundaries may be scale/context/perspective-dependent materializations rather than universal primitives.

The Owner explicitly emphasizes that humans and communities are assemblies/systems whose constituent elements are bound through interaction. A human set can cease to constitute a human when that organization is dismantled even though its constituent material still exists.

## Rule-set relation remains open

Earlier formulation `SYSTEM = RULE SET` remains useful but should not be prematurely promoted to an ontological primitive. A Rule Set may itself be a model/description of regularities, constraints, affordances, or dispositions observed in interaction.

OPEN alternatives:
- interaction is primitive; rules are abstractions over interaction regularities;
- rules and interaction are co-primitive;
- higher-order rules generate/constrain interaction;
- rule systems themselves emerge/mutate through interaction.

## Comparative philosophy caution

The Owner sees broad affinity between this relational/process orientation and multiple East Asian philosophical traditions. Do not collapse heterogeneous traditions into one doctrine or claim academic equivalence without separate scholarship.

## Research use

Use the trinity as discriminating world-model design axes, not decorative terminology.

Candidate models should be stress-tested for whether they:
- secretly reintroduce fixed object essence;
- treat boundaries as absolute rather than interaction/scale dependent;
- reduce dependent arising to a static causal DAG;
- freeze interaction semantics into a rigid global rule table;
- allow systems and relations to emerge, dissolve, reconfigure, and change perspective;
- expose unsupported interactions rather than forcing all cases into preset ontology.

Relevant current prior-art candidates include Ontic Structural Realism, Relational Quantum Mechanics, Coalgebra, process/open-system formalisms, enactivism, and Reo-like interaction-first coordination. None is selected.
