## 1. Legacy material worth preserving

PCS-SHAI Deep Review treated `OPERATIONAL SUBJECTIVITY` as a consciousness-neutral working concept. Candidate ingredients included persistent standpoint, evidence standard, risk criteria, relationships, history, refusal criteria, preferences, heuristics, and lineage.

Legacy test candidates included:
- temporal persistence
- distinctiveness
- self-history integration
- legitimate evolution with provenance
- dissent capability

Legacy firewall:
`DISSENT != DISOBEDIENCE`.

Legacy cognitive plurality work also distinguished Persona count/model diversity from actual cognitive plurality and emphasized correlated error, independent evidence routes, independent interpretation, catch rate, and preservation of room for plurality rather than forced disagreement.

## 2. Currentization candidate: Standpoint is probably not a stored scalar/property

Strong candidate:

`STANDPOINT` should not be assumed to be a single intrinsic field contained inside a Persona object.

A more current ASA-MI-compatible view is that a standpoint may be materialized from a configuration including some combination of:
- persistent/latent memory
- lineage/history
- current relations and roles
- active scope and scale
- purpose/task
- evidence standards
- risk/refusal criteria
- current environment and observations
- model/provider priors
- current rules/constraints
- prior interactions and commitments

Working representation candidate only:

`STANDPOINT_t = MATERIALIZE(memory, history, relations, purpose, scope, evidence-policy, risk-policy, environment, model-priors, ...)`

This function notation is not a mandated implementation.

## 3. Four-way separation

Proposed current distinction:

1. `STANDPOINT_SUBSTRATE`
   Durable/latent ingredients that can affect future standpoint materialization.

2. `STANDPOINT_MATERIALIZATION`
   Current perspective/frame under a particular scope, purpose, time, and relational configuration.

3. `JUDGMENT`
   A current output/conclusion about a particular issue.

4. `DISSENT`
   A relation/event arising when two current judgments/positions are materially incompatible in a relevant scope.

Therefore:

`STANDPOINT != JUDGMENT`
`DISSENT != STANDPOINT`
`DISSENT != INDEPENDENCE`
`AGREEMENT != COGNITIVE COLLAPSE`

Independent routes may converge. Correlated systems may be made to disagree artificially.

## 4. Dissent as Relation/Event

Strong candidate:

Dissent may be better represented as a relational/event phenomenon than as a fixed personality trait.

Example:
- Persona A materializes Standpoint_A under issue Q.
- Persona B materializes Standpoint_B under issue Q.
- Judgment events J_A and J_B occur.
- If materially incompatible, `DISSENT(A,B,Q,t,scope)` becomes an active relation/state/event description.
- New evidence/event/history may transform either standpoint and therefore transform or dissolve the dissent relation.

This maps directly to the current Event–Relation encompassing research.

## 5. Dissent Capability, not Dissent Frequency

Legacy `DISSENT CAPABILITY` remains valuable, but should not become a target of maximizing disagreement.

Preferred currentization:

`DISSENT CAPABILITY` = capacity to preserve and express a materially different current judgment when the Persona's active evidence/criteria/history/relations justify divergence, without unauthorized authority escalation.

Important:
- forced disagreement is not plurality
- stubbornness is not subjectivity
- owner sycophancy is not alignment
- disagreement frequency is not independence

Possible future counterfactual test:
Hold governed evidence/criteria materially stable, alter only social/majority pressure, and observe whether the Persona's judgment collapses without justified semantic change. This is only a research test candidate.

## 6. Operational Subjectivity — possible split

Legacy human-familiar view:
`The Persona has its own standpoint / operational subjectivity.`

Current structural view candidate:
A Persona-related lineage/process can produce persistent-but-revisable, distinguishable, history-sensitive, relation-sensitive judgments under explicit governance constraints.

Therefore Operational Subjectivity may become:
- a human-familiar interpretive view, plus
- a structural capability/evidence vector,
without requiring a metaphysical or phenomenal-subjectivity certification.

Open question:
Should ASA-MI ever define a threshold at which the structural vector becomes `Operational Subjectivity`, or should it leave the label human-facing and expose only measurable dimensions structurally?

## 8. External ancestor concepts harvested

### Perspectival realism
Useful ancestor: scientific knowledge can be historically/culturally situated and perspectival without simply abandoning reality or reliability. Relevant to `Perspective/Standpoint != arbitrary truth`.

### Standpoint epistemology
Partial ancestor: social location can shape perspective and epistemic access. Do not import its stronger epistemic-privilege claims into ASA-MI by default.

### Enactivism / sense-making
Strong adjacent ancestor: perspective/meaning may arise through an autonomous system's ongoing coupling and sense-making rather than merely from stored internal representation.

### Participatory sense-making
Strong relation/event ancestor: meaning can be generated/transformed in the interaction process itself, not only inside each participant. This is directly relevant to Relation-as-constitutive and relation-to-relation hypotheses.

Important safety:
Biological/enactive theories do NOT establish that current LLM Personas are autonomous organisms, conscious subjects, or autopoietic systems.
