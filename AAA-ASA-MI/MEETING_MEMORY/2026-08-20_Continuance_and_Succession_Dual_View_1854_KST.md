## Owner Clarification

The legacy idea `Persona object changes/evolves` should be treated as a shallower/familiar representation that was later developed and deepened, not simply discarded.

Where conceptual lineage exists, preserve explicit mappings between legacy and current thought.

Two views may coexist:

### 1. CONTINUANCE VIEW / 계속성 관점
Human-familiar and operationally familiar representation:
- the same Persona continues and changes;
- `I am still me`;
- a relationship continues while evolving;
- continuity/continuance may be socially, cognitively, legally, narratively, or operationally useful.

This view does not by itself establish metaphysical identity.

`계속성` is preferred over `연속성` because it does not strongly imply mathematically uninterrupted continuous identity.

### 2. SUCCESSION VIEW / 계승성 관점
Structural current ASA-MI candidate:
- material change may create a distinct successor instance;
- predecessor/successor relations and lineage are explicit;
- `SUCCESSION != IDENTITY`;
- fork, merge, fission, recomposition and asymmetric absorption remain natural;
- memory, commitments, relations, rules and authority may be transferred selectively rather than automatically.

`계승성` is preferred over `승계성` for the generic structural concept because Korean `승계` often implies transfer of rights/status/authority. Authority remains a separate Relation in ASA-MI.

## Persona Continuity Envelope Mapping

Legacy `Persona Continuity Envelope` should be preserved as a legacy/familiar concept and mapped rather than overwritten.

Candidate dual mapping:

- `PERSONA CONTINUANCE ENVELOPE`: criteria under which a human/system may reasonably treat successor materializations as the same continuing Persona for a given purpose.
- `PERSONA SUCCESSION RECORD / ENVELOPE`: exact structural lineage, inherited/rematerialized state, authority rebinding, provider/model changes, divergence, fork/merge provenance.

Therefore:

`CONTINUANCE VIEW != SUCCESSION FACT`

and

`SUCCESSION FACT != HUMAN-PERCEIVED CONTINUANCE`.

A succession relation does not automatically imply that all successors should be treated as the same continuing Persona. Forks are the clearest example.

## Self / Bundle Mapping

The same dual-view pattern may apply to Self and Bundle:

- familiar projection: a persistent `I`, same Self, same Persona, same relationship;
- structural representation: relational/compositional materializations linked by succession.

Do not classify the familiar view merely as false or illusion. It may remain useful even if the structural model does not assert strict identity.

## Legacy Mapping Rule

When a genuine development path exists:

`LEGACY FAMILIAR VIEW`
→ preserve original historical meaning
→ add lineage link
→ map to current structural candidate
→ preserve familiar projection where useful.

`LEGACY != CURRENT`
`LINK != RETROACTIVE REWRITE`
`FAMILIAR CONTINUANCE != STRUCTURAL IDENTITY`
