## 1. Owner Clarification — Current Task Is Mapping

The immediate task is to map legacy concepts to currentized concepts when succession/lineage exists.

The legacy whitepaper may intentionally or naturally be closer to the ordinary human viewpoint.

The current ASA-MI model has moved toward a deeper structural / world-proximate representation partly because of a working implementation hypothesis:

> A model that is closer to the currently understood structure of the world may be easier or more robust to implement.

This remains a HYPOTHESIS, not an established truth.

## 2. Two Representation/View Directions

### A. HUMAN-FAMILIAR VIEW
Examples:
- a Persona is the same Persona and changes over time;
- a Self is experienced as one continuing self;
- a Bundle can be spoken of as a coherent whole;
- a boundary/membrane can be understood as a familiar interface around a human-centered unit.

This view may be cognitively, socially, operationally, and experientially valuable even if deeper implementation does not literally use a persistent identical object.

### B. STRUCTURAL / CURRENT ASA-MI VIEW
Examples:
- discrete materialized instances connected by SUCCESSION;
- RELATIONAL CONSTITUTION;
- membership/boundary/authority as separable relation families;
- materialization by scope/scale/context;
- persistent/latent memory versus working/materialized memory;
- event/relation/process models.

The structural view is currently favored for implementation research because it may avoid false permanent-identity assumptions and may fit replay, fork/merge, scale, and change more naturally.

## 3. Core Mapping Rule

Currentization should preserve explicit mappings such as:

- `LEGACY Persona object changes over time`
  ↔ `CURRENT discrete Persona instances connected by succession`

- `LEGACY Persona Continuity Envelope`
  ↔ `CURRENT succession structure + human-facing continuity projection/envelope`

- `LEGACY Self / Self Bundle`
  ↔ `CURRENT relational/compositional materialized Self instance`, while retaining a human-familiar Self view when useful

- `LEGACY Boundary / Membrane`
  ↔ `CURRENT relation/event/coupling-governance surface`, while retaining the membrane metaphor as a human-familiar view if useful

- `LEGACY persistent identity language`
  ↔ `CURRENT lineage/succession/materialization structure`, without forcing humans to interact using only low-level structural terms

These mappings are lineage links, NOT retroactive rewrites of the legacy source.

---

## 4. Important Distinction — Two Views, Not Necessarily Two Ontologies

The project should not prematurely create two separate canonical ontologies.

A more conservative candidate is:

> One deeper structural model may support one or more human-familiar projections/views.

The human-facing view may intentionally preserve continuity, selfhood, persona language, and familiar metaphors even when the structural layer uses succession, relation, event, and materialization.

Whether the dual-view design eventually becomes two formal layers, adapters, projections, schemas, or merely a conceptual mapping remains OPEN.

## 5. Objective Candidate — HUMAN FAMILIARITY / HUMAN-LEGIBLE EXPERIENCE

Owner explicitly states that closeness/familiarity to humans is itself part of the project's purpose.

Working objective candidate:

> Even if ASA-MI uses deeper structural representations for implementation, the resulting system should remain highly familiar, intelligible, and experientially compatible with human self-understanding and ordinary human interaction.

This objective is NOT merely UI convenience.

Potential dimensions include:
- human conceptual familiarity;
- intuitive continuity;
- understandable self/persona metaphors;
- low translation burden between structural model and human experience;
- ability for humans to reason about, inspect, and govern their extended cognitive system without needing to adopt the system's low-level ontology.

Canonical wording and weighting remain OPEN.

## 6. Relation to Human Sovereignty Objective

Human familiarity is distinct from sovereignty but may support it.

A structurally correct system that humans cannot understand, relate to, or reason about may weaken practical governance.

Candidate relationship:

`HUMAN-FAMILIARITY` may be an enabling objective for `HUMAN COGNITIVE SOVEREIGN EXPANSION`.

Do NOT collapse the two objectives.
