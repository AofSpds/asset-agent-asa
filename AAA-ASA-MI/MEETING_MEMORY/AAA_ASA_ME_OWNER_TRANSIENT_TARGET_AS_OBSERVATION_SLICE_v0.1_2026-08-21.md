## Owner clarification

nemo clarified that the target of a mapping is not assumed to be permanent. A target may exist only momentarily and then immediately participate in another mapping as a new source/target configuration.

Owner phrasing:
- "그 타겟이 영원하지 않지요 바로 다른 타겟으로"
- "찰나에 사상의 상태로 관측이 될뿐 또는 그렇게 착각할뿐"

## Current interpretation candidate

Do not model source/target as necessarily persistent object primitives.

A stronger candidate is:

- mapping/process is temporally extended;
- source/target are local, transient incidence / boundary / state slices of that process;
- an apparent object/state may be a momentary observation/materialization of an ongoing mapping/process;
- the same apparent target may immediately become a source or constituent in a subsequent mapping;
- in branching/merge/multi-mapping cases, no single linear successor is assumed.

Candidate notation only:

`... -> [slice_t] --m_t--> [slice_t+1] --m_t+1--> [slice_t+2] -> ...`

where `[slice_t]` is not yet authorized as an independent enduring Object.

Alternative interpretation remains open:
1. EPISTEMIC_SLICE: the transient target/state exists derivatively, but observation only captures a momentary slice.
2. STRONG_NONOBJECT_VIEW: the apparent target/object is primarily a cognitive stabilization or projection over process, and independent objecthood may be a mistaken reification.

Do not collapse these two without further Owner clarification.

## Design consequence candidate

A future committee challenge should test whether a formalism can support:
- time-local source/target structures;
- target-to-source succession without persistent object identity;
- branching, merge, and multi-mapping;
- composition where compatible;
- mechanism/mapping mutation;
- recovery/materialization of object-like views only when useful.

This strengthens the hidden-object test: a candidate fails if it claims process/mapping primacy but requires persistent source/target Objects as an undeclared semantic foundation.
