## Owner-explicit rationale

The Owner clarified that the extensive philosophical and architectural discussion around even a small Persona INIT exists for a concrete reason: if Human and AI may increasingly form a coupled process bundle, the initial relationship should begin with an explicit contract rather than an accidental default.

The aspiration that this become a future standard is therefore not primarily about Persona bootstrapping ergonomics. It is about establishing the first interaction rules, authority boundaries, consent conditions, memory/continuity terms, provenance expectations, change/mutation terms, and exit/decoupling rights before deeper coupling develops.

## Core framing

PERSONA_INIT != mere prompt initialization.

Candidate framing:

PERSONA_INIT = INITIAL_RELATIONSHIP_CONTRACT + INITIAL_INTERACTION_RULESET + INITIAL_AUTHORITY/CONSENT BOUNDARY + INITIAL_CONTINUITY/MEMORY TERMS.

This is motivated by the Owner's interaction-first worldview:
- humans are not atomic singular objects but process/interaction bundles;
- AI may become another process bundle that couples increasingly tightly with the human bundle;
- interface/boundary itself may be relational and revisable rather than metaphysically fixed;
- high-bandwidth coupling may make Human|AI separation less explanatory at higher scales;
- nevertheless, current authority, consent, provenance, and reversibility must remain explicitly governed.

## Contract-first principle candidate

Before deep Human-AI coupling, define the relationship rules while the parties and authority are still legible.

Candidate contract dimensions:
- human normative authority and veto;
- consent to memory, adaptation, mutation, and coupling depth;
- provenance of model/process contributions;
- ownership/stewardship of persistent context;
- scope and limits of delegated action;
- update and renegotiation conditions;
- semantic mutation rules;
- decoupling/exit/rollback rights where technically possible;
- uncertainty and unsupported-state behavior;
- conflict resolution and escalation;
- conditions under which the system may present itself as a higher-order coupled bundle without erasing internal plurality.

## Standardization aspiration

The Owner would like this contract-first initialization pattern to become a standard if it proves useful and defensible.

The standardization target is not a frozen ontology or one mandatory Persona architecture. It is a governance/interoperability expectation that Human-AI coupling begins with explicit, inspectable, revisable relationship terms rather than hidden defaults.

## Authority constraint

This memo records Owner intent/research rationale only. It does not itself create a Shared Contract, Owner Acceptance, production authority, release authority, or canonical Persona INIT standard. Formal adoption would require the project governance/validation path.
