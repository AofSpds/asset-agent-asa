## Core clarification

The Owner clarified that the attraction of modeling interaction as two oppositely-directed bundles of mappings comes primarily from the unidirectionality of time.

The key distinction is:

- Temporal direction is forward-only in the current hypothesis.
- Relational direction between participants may be opposite in two mapping bundles.
- Therefore "bidirectional" interaction does NOT imply reverse-time causation.

Candidate form:

- F_t = {f_i}: A_t -> B_{t+Δ_i}
- G_t = {g_j}: B_t -> A_{t+δ_j}
- C_t = Couple(F_t, G_t)

Both F and G advance forward in time while pointing in opposite participant-relative directions.

This makes the current stronger candidate not simply BIDIRECTIONAL_PRIMITIVE, but:

TEMPORALLY_UNIDIRECTIONAL / RELATIONALLY_RECIPROCAL COUPLED MAPPING BUNDLES

## Implications

1. A source/target distinction can remain meaningful locally without implying fixed enduring Objects.
2. A target at one instant may immediately become a source in later mappings.
3. The opposite-direction mapping bundles need not be inverses, symmetric, synchronous, equal in cardinality, or equal in latency.
4. Persona/Human-ASA interaction may be modeled as recurrent, forward-time, reciprocal mapping bundles rather than a single bidirectional arrow.
5. Mechanism mutation remains allowed: F_t -> F_t+1, G_t -> G_t+1, and coupling structure C_t -> C_t+1 may all change.
6. This is a candidate primitive architecture and not a canonical model selection.
