## 0. Trigger

The first 30-minute pilot generated eight model-family seeds very quickly. The Owner identified that allowing immediate promotion from model idea to model candidate is too permissive.

The process must distinguish unrestricted model ideation from admission as a serious MS0 candidate.

## 1. New state distinction

Use at least three research states:

1. MODEL_IDEA
   - unrestricted divergent proposal,
   - may be speculative, partial, derivative, or only a formalism hint,
   - has no tournament standing.

2. MODEL_PROPOSAL
   - an idea developed enough to face an admission gate,
   - must expose its semantic thesis, commitment surface, and testability.

3. SERIOUS_MODEL_CANDIDATE
   - only a proposal that passes the admission gate,
   - eligible for MS0 Main Round / Pressure Test.

Therefore:

MODEL_IDEA != MODEL_PROPOSAL != SERIOUS_MODEL_CANDIDATE

Eight desired tournament entrants means eight SERIOUS_MODEL_CANDIDATES, not eight quickly generated names or model families.

## 2. Recommended funnel

Do NOT begin by demanding exactly eight candidates.

Prefer:
- generate a wider IDEA POOL,
- develop plausible ideas into MODEL_PROPOSALS,
- admit only proposals that satisfy the Gate,
- continue ideation if fewer than the desired serious-candidate count survive.

Suggested research target for Full MS0:
- IDEA_POOL: flexible, approximately 12–20 ideas if useful,
- MODEL_PROPOSALS: approximately 8–12 developed proposals,
- SERIOUS_MODEL_CANDIDATES: target 8, minimum 6 if genuinely distinct qualified candidates cannot be produced.

These numbers are process guidance, not semantic truth.

## 3. Model Proposal Admission Gate

A MODEL_PROPOSAL must satisfy ALL mandatory gates before promotion to SERIOUS_MODEL_CANDIDATE.

### A1. WORLD-MODEL THESIS

The proposal must state what makes it a candidate World Model rather than merely:
- a database pattern,
- data structure,
- runtime architecture,
- inference layer,
- epistemic ledger,
- visualization,
- formal notation,
- generic mathematical language.

It must explain what is modeled and how the world/model state or equivalent is constituted.

### A2. MATERIAL DISTINCTNESS

The proposal must be materially different from already admitted candidates in semantic or computational behavior.

Different terminology, notation, library, storage backend, or mathematical wrapper is insufficient.

It must state:
- nearest existing candidate,
- exact material difference,
- what different prediction/representation/failure behavior follows from that difference.

### A3. COMMITMENT SURFACE EXPLICITNESS

The proposal must expose where ontology or semantic commitment actually resides.

Examples:
- predicates,
- event individuation,
- observation algebra,
- constraint language,
- context topology,
- latent variables/priors,
- actor addresses/protocols,
- composition types,
- another mechanism.

A proposal claiming to be ontology-free fails unless it can show where unavoidable commitments are represented and revised.

### A4. CHANGE / HISTORY SEMANTICS

The proposal must provide a plausible account of change and historical preservation or explicitly justify a different equivalent.

At minimum it must answer:
- what can change,
- what record/history remains,
- whether current interpretation can change without silently rewriting prior evidence/state.

### A5. NON-CLOSURE SEMANTICS

The proposal must show how it can preserve at least one unresolved condition such as:
- UNKNOWN,
- UNDEFINED,
- DISPUTED,
- multiple admissible alternatives,
- another explicit non-closure equivalent.

It must not silently collapse unresolved status to FALSE / ABSENT / null noise.

### A6. BOUNDED OPERATIONALIZATION

The proposal must be operational enough to test.

Before admission, it must provide at least one bounded worked example, pseudocode/formal micro-probe, or executable sketch showing its core semantics.

Pure rhetoric or a purely decorative formalism is insufficient.

### A7. COMMON-QUERY CONTACT

The proposal must demonstrate how it would answer, reject, or reformulate at least three shared questions such as:
- what is represented now,
- what changed,
- what was represented at time T,
- what remains unresolved,
- what differs under another declared context/scope/scale,
- what assumptions were used.

Rejecting a question is allowed only with explicit alternative semantics.

### A8. FALSIFICATION / ABANDONMENT CONDITION

The proposal must state at least one observation or experiment that would materially weaken, redesign, or abandon it.

A model that can explain every possible result after the fact is not admitted.

### A9. ASSUMPTION REGISTER

The proposal must expose its major assumptions and classify them where possible as:
- Owner-explicit,
- inherited research hypothesis,
- model-family assumption,
- Codex/new proposal.

At minimum record:
- assumption,
- why needed,
- what fails if false,
- reversibility.

### A10. REVISION / SUCCESSOR PATH

The proposal must explain how its own semantic commitments could be revised in a successor model without pretending the historical version never existed.

The proposal need not solve migration fully, but must not require retrospective semantic erasure as its normal evolution mechanism.

### A11. NON-TRIVIALITY

The proposal must provide meaningful representational or computational leverage.

It fails admission if it is safe only because it says almost nothing, or if all actual semantics are delegated to another unmodeled layer.

### A12. LOW-LEVEL GENERALITY

The proposal must remain a plausible low-level 한알 World Model candidate rather than a Persona/ASA-specific schema.

Persona/ASA may be used as future stress targets but must not define the proposal's primitive semantics merely for convenience.

## 4. Admission routing

Allowed proposal routing states:

ADMIT_SERIOUS_CANDIDATE
DEVELOP_FURTHER
MERGE_WITH_EXISTING_PROPOSAL
KEEP_AS_COUNTERIDEA
REJECT_CURRENT_ROUND
REVIEW_REQUIRED

These are research routing states only.
They are NOT validation PASS/FAIL states.

## 5. Strong rejection patterns

A proposal should NOT enter the serious-candidate tournament if any of the following remains unresolved at admission time:

- merely a known formalism name with no world-model thesis,
- only a thin wrapper over another candidate,
- no explicit commitment/ontology relocation surface,
- no bounded operational example,
- no abandonment/falsification condition,
- historical reconstruction requires silent rewrite,
- unresolved states collapse to false/absent by default,
- materially indistinguishable from an admitted candidate after a shared scenario,
- depends on Persona/ASA-specific assumptions to function as a world model,
- hides implementation convenience as metaphysical necessity.

## 6. Implication for the first pilot candidates

C01–C08 from the first pilot remain useful MODEL_IDEAS / seed-level MODEL_PROPOSALS.

They must NOT be automatically grandfathered into Full MS0 as serious candidates.

Full MS0 should regenerate or independently elaborate candidate proposals and apply this admission gate without exposing pilot representative rankings during the first independent pass.

Pilot representatives C04/C05 remain quarantined historical observations.
