## Core clarification
Owner states that after repeated reflection, source/target appears closer to the essential structure than a bare arrow alone.

Key Owner intuition:
- A mapping/function is not usefully understood as an untyped bare arrow only; source and target matter intrinsically.
- A target need not be an independently persistent Object.
- Candidate interpretation: the target can be a temporarily materialized/stabilized state of a particular function/mapping as it is cognitively or operationally apprehended at a time/context.
- Therefore source/target may be cuts, states, or compatibility surfaces of an ongoing mapping/process rather than pre-given substance objects.
- Functions/mappings may themselves serve as source or target at higher order.

## Candidate computational translation
Do NOT canonicalize yet. Candidate only:

m_t = (S_t, phi_t, T_t)

where S_t and T_t are not required to be enduring object substances. T_t may be represented as a context/time/scale-dependent materialization of a mapping or mapping-bundle state.

Possible research form:
T_t = MATERIALIZE(mapping_or_bundle, context, scale, observation/interaction conditions, t)

A recurrent/persistent pattern of such source/target materializations may later justify an Object-like view, but Object persistence is not assumed at the primitive layer.

## Important open question
Whether source should be treated symmetrically with target as another temporarily materialized state/condition, or whether source can be a different structural class, remains OPEN and should be elicited rather than assumed.

## Research significance
This yields a strong SPEC_CANDIDATE:
- support typed morphisms / transformations;
- permit source/target positions to be occupied by mappings, mapping-bundles, or materialized mapping states;
- do not require source/target to be enduring primitive Objects;
- permit object-like identity to emerge from persistent/recurrent source-target-composition structure;
- preserve composability semantics without silently reintroducing substance Objects.

No canonical primitive selection is authorized by this memo.
