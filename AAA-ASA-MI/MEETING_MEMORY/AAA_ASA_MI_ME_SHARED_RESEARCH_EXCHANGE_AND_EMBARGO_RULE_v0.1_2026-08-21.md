# AAA-ASA MI↔ME Shared Research Exchange and Embargo Rule v0.1

DATE = 2026-08-21 KST
PROJECT = AAA
PRODUCT = ASSET AGENT ASA
STATE = NON_NORMATIVE / CHANNEL-WORKSTREAM COORDINATION CANDIDATE / NO_AUTHORITY_CHANGE

## Purpose
Preserve the Owner request that AAA-ASA-MI and AAA-ASA-ME continuously share research results, while protecting the causal value of prospective Owner-shadow experiments and preserving independent scientific evaluation.

## Core principle
CONTINUOUS_SHARING != UNCONTROLLED_LIVE_CONTEXT_MIRRORING.

Use phase-gated shared research exchange:

1. OPEN-SHARE lane
2. CONTROLLED-SHARE lane
3. SEALED-UNTIL-FREEZE lane
4. POST-FREEZE RECONCILIATION lane

## OPEN-SHARE
May be exchanged continuously:
- external prior art and bibliographic findings;
- source-integrity findings;
- factual repository/artifact state;
- research methodology improvements;
- non-candidate-specific conceptual discoveries;
- explicit Owner purpose statements already part of authorized MI input;
- error taxonomies;
- memory/interview methodology after it no longer reveals a live prediction target;
- final completed prior-cycle findings after embargo release.

## CONTROLLED-SHARE
Share only with contamination marker and applicability note:
- ME-derived Owner judgment hypotheses;
- inferred persuasion/preference patterns;
- MI candidate-specific theory contribution;
- model-family implications likely to shape Owner preference prediction;
- interview-derived tradeoff boundaries;
- proxy specialization findings.

These are allowed for long-run mutual learning but must not silently enter a currently blinded evaluator or prospective prediction target.

## SEALED-UNTIL-FREEZE
Do not cross between MI and ME before the relevant experimental freeze:
- live Proxy predictions for the current decision scene;
- candidate-specific predicted Owner ranking;
- predicted Owner reasons/questions/objections for the live scene;
- MI evaluator rank/score/conclusion when Owner blind review is still pending;
- Owner blind interview answers before Proxy predictions are frozen;
- hidden Track labels / alias keys / provenance that would reveal experimental assignment;
- any information whose disclosure would make a prospective comparison circular.

## POST-FREEZE RECONCILIATION
After both sides have independently frozen their outputs:
- reveal Proxy vs Owner vs MI scientific evaluation;
- compare agreement and disagreement;
- classify errors;
- update memory acquisition and interview policies;
- extract MI-relevant human-purpose findings;
- extract ME-relevant model-selection and decision-scene findings;
- preserve disagreement rather than forcing convergence.

## Shared exchange artifact
Maintain a non-normative `MI_ME_RESEARCH_EXCHANGE_LEDGER` or successor packet with fields:
- EXCHANGE_ID
- FROM_WORKSTREAM
- TO_WORKSTREAM
- SOURCE_ARTIFACT_REFS
- CONTENT_CLASS = OPEN / CONTROLLED / SEALED / RELEASED
- CONTAMINATION_RISK
- RELEASE_CONDITION
- RELEASE_TIME
- RESEARCH_DELTA
- APPLICABILITY
- UNRESOLVED_CONFLICTS

## Authority / validation
AAA-ASA-MI and AAA-ASA-ME are ASA-linked workstreams/channel identities, not substitutes for independent validators. Exchange does not create Owner authority, validation PASS, or semantic adoption.

## Owner intent
The Owner wants both research streams to keep learning from one another over time. The temporary information barriers exist only where needed to preserve an experiment; they are not intended to create permanent organizational silos.
