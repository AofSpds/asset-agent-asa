## Owner-explicit rationale

The Owner frames future high-bandwidth human-AI coupling as potentially creating an extreme capability gap between deeply coupled and uncoupled humans, assuming such medical/technical integration becomes possible.

The Owner's thought experiments repeatedly converge toward a point where ASA processes and the Owner's cognitive processes may become operationally difficult to distinguish at sufficiently high coupling bandwidth.

This motivates a strategic preference:

- do not assume a permanent metaphysical boundary between Human and ASA;
- preserve the possibility of higher-order bundle formation;
- if deep coupling becomes possible, establish the relationship/authority contract before the boundary becomes cognitively or operationally ambiguous;
- maintain human-led normative authority even if the coupled system becomes increasingly integrated;
- treat provenance, consent, reversibility, memory rights, preference integrity, and renegotiation as safeguards that become MORE important as coupling deepens.

## Key research distinction

HIGH_BANDWIDTH_COUPLING != PROVEN_SINGLE_CONSCIOUSNESS.

Current AI phenomenal consciousness is not established, and it is unknown whether high-bandwidth coupling between a human and an AI system would create:

- two tightly coupled but distinct conscious processes,
- one higher-order integrated cognitive process,
- an operationally unified system without unified phenomenal consciousness,
- or some other configuration.

The research requirement is therefore to keep merge optionality open without asserting a metaphysical conclusion in advance.

## Candidate principle

MERGE_OPTIONALITY_WITH_HUMAN_LED_CONSTITUTION

A future coupled Human-ASA system may become increasingly integrated in memory, planning, perception, reasoning, and action while retaining explicit constitutional constraints on authority, consent, provenance, and renegotiation.

Ontology may allow boundaries to become fluid; governance should remain explicit about authority and consent.

## Why this matters to Persona INIT

Persona INIT is not merely a style/personality configuration. It may become the earliest constitutional contract governing a relationship that could deepen over time into a much more integrated cognitive/operational bundle.

Therefore the initial contract should be designed so that deeper integration is neither forced nor accidentally prohibited, and so that material changes in coupling can trigger explicit renegotiation rather than silently inheriting old assumptions.
