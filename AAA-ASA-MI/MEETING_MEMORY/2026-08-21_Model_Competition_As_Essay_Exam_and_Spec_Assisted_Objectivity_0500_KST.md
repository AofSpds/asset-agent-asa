## 0. Owner clarification

Owner clarified why the project uses a model competition / multiple challenger design instead of relying mainly on a few conversational red-team personas.

The intended experiment is closer to a high-level essay examination than a simple multiple-choice tournament.

Each challenger should:
- deeply understand the current ASA-MI worldview and its reasoning lineage,
- independently form a theoretical/model position,
- debate the worldview from within that shared understanding,
- identify weaknesses, omissions, or missing theoretical space,
- propose a countermodel or enrichment when warranted,
- and argue for preservation, reformulation, or replacement based on purpose and evidence.

## 1. Why model specification helps

The Owner expects the World Model specification and executable/model-level requirements to provide more objective evaluation surfaces than a free-form chat debate alone.

This does NOT mean model specifications create perfect objectivity or ground truth.

Working interpretation:
- open-ended reasoning remains essay-like and theory-rich,
- common model specifications, fixtures, microprobes, derivability checks, replay, assumption registers, and failure conditions provide standardized evidence surfaces,
- these surfaces can constrain rhetorical advantage and make competing positions more comparable,
- qualitative judgment remains necessary for theory contribution, purpose alignment, explanatory gain, and worldview enrichment.

Therefore:

`FREE_FORM_DEBATE_ONLY < ESSAY_LIKE_THEORY_CHALLENGE + COMMON_MODEL_SPEC_EVIDENCE`

for the intended ASA-MI research purpose.

## 2. Why not only three conversational red teams

A small number of conversational adversaries can provide useful criticism, but they risk:
- limited theoretical coverage,
- conversational convergence,
- dependence on rhetoric and local context,
- weak repeatability,
- difficulty separating a strong argument from a strong computational model,
- insufficient evidence for cross-position comparison.

The model competition is intended to create a larger set of isolated independent research positions and then use common specifications/tests to recover comparable evidence without reducing the research to a single scalar ranking.

## 3. Scientific caution

`MODEL_SPEC != OBJECTIVE_TRUTH`.

Model specifications help create objective-like / standardized measurement surfaces, but the choice of specification, fixtures, and evaluation regime can itself bias selection. This is why rubric-sensitivity, held-out fixtures, metamorphic testing, negative controls, and independent challenger positions remain necessary.

The desired end product is not merely a winner. It is:
- stronger World Models,
- a richer Theory Set,
- detected worldview gaps,
- explicit counterarguments,
- and justified worldview deltas.

## 4. Current design implication

Future challenger cohort design should preserve both layers:

1. `ESSAY / DIALECTIC LAYER`
   - deep worldview comprehension
   - independent theory formation
   - attack / defense / advocacy
   - gap discovery and theory contribution

2. `MODEL-SPEC / EVIDENCE LAYER`
   - minimum substantive World Model qualification
   - explicit semantics and commitments
   - native consequences
   - bounded microprobes / replay where applicable
   - common fixtures and adversarial tests
   - assumption and failure surfaces

Neither layer replaces the other.
