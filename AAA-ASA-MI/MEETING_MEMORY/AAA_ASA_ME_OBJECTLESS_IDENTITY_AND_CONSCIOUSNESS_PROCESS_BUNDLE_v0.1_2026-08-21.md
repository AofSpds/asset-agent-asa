## Owner-explicit worldview

- The Owner rejects taking the human as an atomic primitive object merely because human cognition experiences a unitary self.
- The Owner's current modeling intuition is that a human is better viewed as a collection/system of interacting components and rules.
- Consciousness is modeled conceptually as a bundle of processes rather than as a single indivisible object.
- A process is, in the Owner's computational intuition, a chain/composition of functions; functions may map values to values and may also take/return functions, allowing higher-order chained transformation.
- The Owner's world-model emphasis remains interaction-first: stable higher-level entities may be materialized from recurring interaction/process structure rather than assumed as primitive substances.

## Objectless category-theory clarification

In objectless formulations of category theory, ordinary objects need not be primitive. Arrows/morphisms and composition can be taken as primitive, while what ordinary category theory calls an object can be represented by an identity morphism.

For f: A -> B:
- f o id_A = f
- id_B o f = f

The identity morphism is not a metaphysical essence or soul. It is a neutral compositional arrow that behaves as the source/target identity needed for legal composition. Thus an 'object' can be reconstructed as a compositional locus/type-equivalence role without being a primitive substance-like entity.

Research implication:
- OBJECT_PRIMITIVE can be removed from the formal vocabulary;
- COMPOSITIONAL_IDENTITY / TYPING / COMPATIBILITY still reappears in some form;
- a persistent human/persona identity could be investigated as a stable process/interaction locus or equivalence class, rather than as an immutable object core.

## Important scientific caution

`CONSCIOUSNESS = PROCESS_BUNDLE` is currently an Owner modeling hypothesis, not a neuroscience claim established here.
`PROCESS = FUNCTION_CHAIN` is a useful computational special case, but general real-world processes may require stateful, stochastic, concurrent, nondeterministic, or interactive arrows/relations rather than pure deterministic functions.

## Candidate formal direction

Prefer a generalized primitive family:
`ARROW / TRANSFORMATION / PROCESS / INTERACTION`
with pure functions as one important special case.

A possible recursive view:
`processes compose -> relatively stable interaction nexus -> higher-level system/persona materialization -> further interaction -> process/rule change`.
