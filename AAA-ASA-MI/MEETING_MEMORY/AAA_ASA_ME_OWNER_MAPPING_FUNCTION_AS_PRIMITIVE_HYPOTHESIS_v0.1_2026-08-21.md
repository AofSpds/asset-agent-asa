## Owner statement
nemo is intentionally continuing to push the idea of function / mapping / 사상 as a possible computational primitive for the World Model. The question is not whether ordinary software can use functions, but whether an interaction/process-oriented substrate can treat mapping/transformation itself as more fundamental than Object.

## Required distinction
1. Set-theoretic function `f: A -> B` is not objectless by itself, because domain/codomain and value sets are already presupposed.
2. Lambda-calculus / combinatory formulations show that data-like values can be encoded functionally and that application/transformation can be foundational, but this does not automatically solve world-model identity, concurrency, stochasticity, causality, or self-modifying mechanisms.
3. Objectless/category-theoretic formulations are especially relevant because morphisms and composition can be treated as primitive-like, with object-like structure recoverable through identity/compositional structure.
4. For AAA's target, `function` may be too narrow if interactions are stochastic, stateful, concurrent, asynchronous, partial, open, or mechanism-mutating. Broader primitive candidates remain `Morphism / Process / Interaction / Transformation / Mapping`.
5. A central unresolved requirement is mechanism mutation: not only `x -> x'` under fixed `f`, but `f -> g`; and potentially higher-order change where mappings transform mappings.

## Current research question
Can a World Model begin from mapping / transformation / interaction primitives and recover Object, Boundary, Identity, Interface, and Persona as derived/materialized structures without merely hiding those objects in types, domains, codomains, or meta-rules?

## Fail conditions
- Objects are nominally removed but reappear as fixed hidden domains/types/boundaries.
- Mapping is deterministic/pure only and cannot express open, stochastic, concurrent, stateful or partial interaction.
- Mechanism change requires an immutable external meta-rule that simply relocates rigidity.
- Derived identity cannot support continuity, differentiation, merge, or re-materialization at multiple scales.

## Research discipline
Do not canonize `FUNCTION_IS_THE_PRIMITIVE`. Treat it as a strong candidate hypothesis. Compare ordinary functions, higher-order functions, lambda/combinatory foundations, objectless category formulations, process/morphism formalisms, and interaction-first models under the same microprobes.
