## Purpose
Provide a small operating guide for discovering, re-harvesting, mapping, and currentizing hypotheses without prematurely turning them into requirements, ontology, or final truth.

This is NOT a Requirement, Design Contract, Frozen Artifact, Validation Receipt, or Final Truth.

## Working Rules

1. **LEGACY FIRST / REUSE BEFORE INVENTION**
   - Search existing philosophy, science, mathematics, cognitive science, computer science, standards, prior project artifacts, and other legacy before inventing a new abstraction.
   - New R&D is for material gaps, not novelty for its own sake.

2. **CANDIDATE BEFORE CONCLUSION**
   - Prefer generating many explicit candidates first.
   - Weight, challenge, compare, prototype, and currentize later.
   - `CURRENT_HIGH_WEIGHT != FINAL_TRUTH`.

3. **SOURCE / ORIGIN LABELING**
   - Distinguish at least:
     - OWNER_EXPLICIT / OWNER_RECALLED
     - LEGACY_REHARVEST
     - ASA_INFERRED_FROM_PATTERN
     - ASA_NEW_PROPOSAL
     - ADVERSARIAL_COUNTER_HYPOTHESIS
   - Do not silently promote inference into Owner-held belief.

4. **LEGACY != CURRENT**
   - Preserve historical wording and meaning.
   - When legacy and current thought connect, add lineage/mapping instead of rewriting history.
   - `LINK != RETROACTIVE REWRITE`.

5. **MAP HUMAN-FAMILIAR AND STRUCTURAL VIEWS**
   - Preserve human-familiar concepts when useful even if deeper structural representations exist.
   - Map, where possible:
     `LEGACY/HUMAN-FAMILIAR VIEW <-> CURRENT STRUCTURAL VIEW <-> HUMAN-FACING PROJECTION`.
   - Human familiarity is an Objective concern; structural realism/implementation proximity is a modeling/implementation hypothesis.

6. **KEEP COMPETING ABSTRACTIONS ALIVE**
   - Do not force Relation-first, Event-first, Entity-first, Process-first, function, graph, tree, or other representation to win early.
   - If a broader model can contain useful distinctions, preserve the distinctions and test the encompassing model.

7. **WEIGHT, DO NOT CANONIZE PREMATURELY**
   - Candidate states may include high-weight, supported, challenged, review-required, open, or similar working judgments.
   - Vocabulary itself is not frozen.
   - INIT consumes a sufficiently strong current candidate set, not a completed ontology.

8. **DISAGREEMENT AND FAILURE ARE DATA**
   - Preserve counterexamples, minority views, implementation failures, changed votes/confidence, and rejected hypotheses.
   - Codex/adversarial review produces research evidence, not Validation PASS.

9. **IMPLEMENTATION COST MATTERS, BUT DOES NOT DEFINE REALITY**
   - Prefer the cheapest adequate bounded representation for INIT/prototypes.
   - `SEMANTIC RELATIONALITY != UNIVERSAL EXPENSIVE GRAPH`.
   - A tree/table/event stream/function/hybrid may implement a richer semantic hypothesis if preservation is demonstrated.

10. **P0 DISCIPLINE**
    - No candidate is final by default.
    - Non-blocking unknowns may remain OPEN.
    - Aim for `SUFFICIENT_TO_RUN`, then use execution evidence to update weights and successors.

## Minimal Harvest Record

For an important candidate, preserve when useful:
- candidate statement;
- origin/source;
- ancestor concepts / references;
- current judgment / relative weight;
- links to related or competing hypotheses;
- legacy-to-current mapping if applicable;
- main attack surface / counterexamples;
- implementation implications and cost risks;
- OPEN questions;
- evidence/research/prototype refs when available.
