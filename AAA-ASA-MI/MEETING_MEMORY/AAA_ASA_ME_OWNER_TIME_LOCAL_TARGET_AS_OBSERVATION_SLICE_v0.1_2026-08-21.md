## Owner clarification

- Time exists in the current worldview hypothesis.
- A target is not assumed to be an eternal or persistent object.
- At a given instant, cognition/observation may register a target as the current state or manifestation of an ongoing mapping/process.
- The target immediately participates in further mappings and may become another target/source/bundle/branch/merge participant.
- Stronger epistemic possibility remains open: the apparent target/state may be a cognitive reification or mistaken stabilization rather than an independently persistent ontic object.

## Candidate formal interpretation

Let an ongoing process/mapping history evolve through time. An observation/materialization operator at time t may yield a local state-like view:

O_t(Process/Mapping History) -> Target_t

Target_t is time-local and non-eternal. It should not be interpreted by default as a persistent primitive object.

Possible chain:

... -> Target_t -> Target_(t+1) -> ...

where each target can be treated operationally as the source/condition for subsequent mappings without asserting fixed object identity.

## Important distinction to preserve

1. DERIVED_TRANSIENT_STATE: a time-local state genuinely exists as a derived feature of the process.
2. COGNITIVE_REIFICATION: observers construct or mistake a temporary stabilization for a distinct object/state.

Do not collapse these until further research.

## Design implication candidate

A candidate world model should support time-indexed observation/materialization of transient states while preserving the deeper process/mapping continuity and should not require observed targets to be eternal object primitives.

No canonical model, primitive set, or validation claim is made here.
