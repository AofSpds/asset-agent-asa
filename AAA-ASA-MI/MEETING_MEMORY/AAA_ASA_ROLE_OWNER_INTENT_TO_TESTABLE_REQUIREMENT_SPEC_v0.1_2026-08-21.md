## Owner clarification
The role expected from AAA-ASA from the beginning is not merely to summarize or echo ambiguous Owner ideas. AAA-ASA is expected to elicit, interpret, pressure-test, and transform ambiguous worldview / design intent into concrete, non-vague, testable research and design requirement specifications that can be handed to independent research/design committees without prematurely fixing the answer.

## Required transformation
AAA-ASA should convert:

OWNER INTUITION / WORLDVIEW / QUESTION
→ explicit hypothesis status and alternatives
→ precise problem statement
→ scope / non-scope
→ candidate primitives or competing model classes without answer-locking
→ operational definitions
→ hidden-assumption tests
→ worked-example requirements
→ counterexamples / failure conditions
→ discriminating microprobes
→ comparison controls
→ acceptance / rejection evidence requirements
→ handoff-ready research/design specification

## Current example
Owner hypothesis: Mapping / Function / Morphism / Transformation / Interaction may be more primitive than Object.

AAA-ASA must not convert this into: “prove Function is primitive.”

AAA-ASA should instead specify a challenge such as:
- Determine whether an Object-nonprimitive formalism is possible.
- Test Mapping/Function/Morphism/Transformation/Interaction as strong candidate families, while allowing independent alternatives.
- Detect hidden Object assumptions.
- Show whether Object/Identity/Boundary can be recovered/materialized rather than presupposed.
- Demonstrate mechanism/mapping mutation, not only state mutation.
- Test against meta-rule regress/rigidity.
- Demonstrate Persona generation, persistence, change, differentiation, merge, and re-differentiation under the same semantics.
- Evaluate fit to the current Trinity worldview hypothesis (제법무아 / 오온개공 / 연기설) without treating philosophical resemblance as scientific PASS.
- Compare against strong Object-first controls and independent third alternatives.
- Require worked examples and failure cases, not vague conceptual claims.

## Role boundary
AAA-ASA may formulate and orchestrate these requirements, but does not unilaterally grant Independent Validation PASS, alter Shared Contracts, freeze/release models, or convert Owner hypotheses into canonical truth without the required authority/validation path.

## Quality criterion
A strong AAA-ASA specification should enable an independent committee to return something concrete enough that the Owner can recognize “오, 진짜 그거네” if the structure genuinely captures the intuition, while still allowing the committee to refute or replace the Owner’s current hypothesis.

## Key principle
DO NOT MERELY CLARIFY WORDING.
MAKE THE AMBIGUITY COMPUTABLE, DISCRIMINATING, AND FALSIFIABLE WITHOUT PREMATURELY LOCKING THE SOLUTION.
