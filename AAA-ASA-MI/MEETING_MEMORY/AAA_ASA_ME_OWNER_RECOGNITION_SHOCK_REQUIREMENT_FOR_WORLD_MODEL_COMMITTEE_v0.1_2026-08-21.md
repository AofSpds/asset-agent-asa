# AAA-ASA-ME — Recognition-Shock Requirement for World Model Committee v0.1

STATUS = OWNER_EXPLICIT / RESEARCH_REQUIREMENT_CANDIDATE / NON_NORMATIVE / NOT_VALIDATED

## Owner intent
A committee response must not remain at the level of vague philosophical compatibility. The current Function/Mapping-first idea is an intentionally broad hypothesis. The useful result is a concrete formal or computational construction that makes the Owner react: "오, 진짜 그거네" because it exposes a mechanism that directly explains the worldview and Persona lifecycle requirements.

## Required evidence shape
A candidate should, where applicable:
1. State the exact primitive set and semantics.
2. Show whether Object/Identity/Boundary are primitive, hidden assumptions, or derived constructions.
3. Provide at least one minimal worked construction showing object-like identity/materialization from the proposed substrate.
4. Show a Persona lifecycle path including persistence/change/differentiation/merge/re-differentiation without ad hoc special-case semantics if claimed native.
5. Show whether mapping/mechanism itself can change, not only state or parameters.
6. Identify where rigidity/invariants remain and whether meta-rule regress is merely displaced.
7. Compare against a strong Object-first control and at least one independent alternative.
8. State explicit failure conditions and what evidence would falsify the candidate's claimed fit.

## Recognition criterion
The target is not rhetorical resemblance to 제법무아 / 오온개공 / 연기설. The target is a sufficiently explicit mechanism, formalism, or executable micro-model such that the mapping from the substrate to the worldview requirements and Persona lifecycle is inspectable and non-hand-wavy.

## Authority note
Mapping/Function/Morphism/Transformation-first remains an Owner hypothesis, not an approved design answer. The committee is authorized to reject it and propose a better primitive architecture.
