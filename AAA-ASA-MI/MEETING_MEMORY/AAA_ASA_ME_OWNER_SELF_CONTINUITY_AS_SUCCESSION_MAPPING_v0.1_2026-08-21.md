## Owner statement

Owner recalls previously using the concept of **succession / inheritance (계승)** for continuity of self.

The Owner states that the self changes continuously along the time axis and can be thought of as a mapping:

`T0 me -> T1 me`

The key implication is that continuity need not mean strict identity or preservation of a fixed essence. A current self-state/configuration may succeed to a later self-state/configuration through a temporally and causally connected transformation.
