## Hypothesis

Owner proposes that even directed mapping may not be the deepest primitive. A more fundamental candidate may be bidirectional interaction / coupling, with one-way source→target mappings emerging as time-local, causal, observational, or analytical projections of that interaction.

Candidate forms:
- coupled interaction I(A,B)
- joint transformation (A_t,B_t) -> (A_{t+1},B_{t+1})
- bundles of directionally distinct mappings {A->B, B->A, ...}
- directed arrows as derived views/projections rather than primitive ontology

## Important distinction

"Bidirectional" should not be prematurely encoded as exactly two opposite arrows. The deeper candidate may be a joint interaction/coupling relation from which directional decompositions can be derived when useful.

## World-model relevance

This may fit:
- dependent origination / interdependence orientation,
- non-primitive object boundaries,
- observer/observed co-change,
- persona co-adaptation, differentiation, merge, re-differentiation,
- Human–ASA higher-order bundle formation.
