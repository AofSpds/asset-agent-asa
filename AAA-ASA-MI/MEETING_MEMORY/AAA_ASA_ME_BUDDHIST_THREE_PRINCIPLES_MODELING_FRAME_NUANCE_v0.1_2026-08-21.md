## Owner-explicit clarification

For the current world-model construction problem, the Owner gives primary conceptual weight to:
- 제법무아
- 공(空)
- 연기(緣起)

The Owner treats 사성제 and 팔정도 as peripheral to this specific modeling exercise. This is a modeling-priority statement, not a doctrinal ranking.

The Owner also clarified that calling this worldview 'radical' may reflect a Western substance/object-first framing more than the intrinsic character of Buddhist thought.

## Current computational mapping candidate

- 제법무아 -> do not assume a primitive, self-subsisting Object/Identity.
- 공 -> do not assign complete intrinsic essence/semantics independent of relation, condition, context, and perspective.
- 연기 -> model arising, change, persistence, and disappearance through conditional interaction and dependence.

Candidate synthesis:
`RULE / CONDITION / INTERACTION`
-> `LOCAL OR DISTRIBUTED TRANSITION`
-> `RELATION / EVENT / OBJECT / PERSONA MATERIALIZATION`
-> `NEW CONDITIONS / INTERACTIONS`

This is a research hypothesis only. It must compete with object-first, relation-first, event-first, process-first, coalgebraic, coordination, and other variants.

## Important caution

Do not collapse the three Buddhist concepts into one proposition. In particular:
- 제법무아 is not simply 'nothing exists';
- 공 is not semantic arbitrariness or nihilism;
- 연기 is not identical to a simple deterministic causal graph;
- Buddhist traditions differ in interpretation, and the project should use these concepts as design inspirations, not as claims of faithful doctrinal implementation.

## Research implication

The strongest project-relevant interpretation is not merely `OBJECTS HAVE RULES`, but potentially `OBJECTS MAY BE DERIVED MATERIALIZATIONS OF RULE/RELATION/INTERACTION STRUCTURES`.

The key falsification risk is that rigidity merely migrates from object definitions into a fixed rule table. Therefore interaction/rule flexibility, higher-order rule composition, capability boundaries, history/provenance, and controlled successor mutation must remain explicit test axes.
