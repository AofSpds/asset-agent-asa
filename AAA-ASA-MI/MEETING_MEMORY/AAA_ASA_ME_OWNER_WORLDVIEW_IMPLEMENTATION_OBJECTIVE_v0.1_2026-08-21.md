## Current working interpretation

1. OBJECT IS NOT REQUIRED AS A PRIMITIVE.
   Objects, humans, communities, identities, interfaces, events, and relations may be materialized views over deeper process/interaction structure.

2. INTERACTION / PROCESS / MAPPING ARE PRIMARY CANDIDATES.
   Candidate substrate language should privilege interaction, process, transformation, composition, compatibility, history, and condition/context over intrinsic object essence.

3. SYSTEM MAY BE A BUNDLE.
   A system may be treated as a relatively stable bundle/organization of interacting processes rather than a singular atomic entity.

4. HUMAN IS NOT ASSUMED SINGULAR.
   Human/persona/consciousness may be modeled as a process bundle; this is a modeling hypothesis, not an established neuroscience claim.

5. MAPPING ITSELF MAY CHANGE.
   Not only state x_t -> x_{t+1}, but mechanism/function/mapping f_t -> f_{t+1} may change through interaction.
   This requires distinguishing state mutation, parameter mutation, topology/interface mutation, and mechanism mutation.

6. INTERFACE IS RELATIONAL.
   UI/API/ASA interface should not be reified as a permanent metaphysical boundary. Interface may be a temporary interaction surface materialized by relation/context/purpose.

7. OBJECT-LIKE IDENTITY MAY EMERGE.
   Objectless/process-first formalisms may still recover identity-like/composition-compatible structures without requiring intrinsic immutable essence.

8. HUMAN-FRIENDLY VIEW, MACHINE-NATIVE SUBSTRATE.
   The substrate may be difficult for humans to manipulate directly. ASA may translate/project it into human-friendly object/relation/event/API/narrative views without reifying those projections as primitive truth.

9. HUMAN–ASA MERGE OPTIONALITY.
   Human and ASA should not be permanently ontologized as two atomic entities. Future high-bandwidth coupling may be better modeled as a higher-order process bundle while preserving provenance, consent, and human-led normative authority.

10. ONTOLOGY VS GOVERNANCE.
    Candidate principle:
    ONTOLOGY_BOUNDARIES_WEAK / GOVERNANCE_AUTHORITY_CONSENT_PROVENANCE_STRONG.

    Candidate companion principle:
    CONSTITUTION_BEFORE_COUPLING.

## Research discipline

Do not reward a model merely because it sounds philosophically aligned.

Discriminating tests should include:
- Can it model a system without primitive Object ontology?
- Can identity-like continuity emerge without hidden immutable identity?
- Can relations/interactions change without moving rigidity into a fixed rule table?
- Can the mapping/mechanism itself change, not just state or parameters?
- Can a genuinely unseen interaction be represented, rejected as unsupported, or trigger a successor model without semantic cheating?
- Can multiple scales/views be materialized from the same substrate?
- Can provenance and replay survive dynamic mechanism change?
- Can ASA generate stable human-friendly projections while preserving uncertainty, scope, and invalidation conditions?
- Can Human–ASA coupling deepen without conflating operational integration with normative authority?
