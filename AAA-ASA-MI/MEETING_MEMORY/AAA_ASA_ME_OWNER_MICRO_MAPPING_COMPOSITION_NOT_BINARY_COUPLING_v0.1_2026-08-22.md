## Owner clarification

The prior binary framing `A <-> B`, or even two opposite-direction mapping bundles joined by one implicit coupling function, may itself be an over-compression inconsistent with the intended worldview.

Owner's current intuition:

- Reality should not be assumed to consist of two pre-given sides coupled by one global binary coupling function.
- What appears at low resolution as `A <-> B` may instead be an abstraction over innumerable small directed mappings/interactions.
- Those small mappings can be composed into larger composite mappings/functions.
- A higher-scale reciprocal relationship may therefore be a materialized/compressed view of a dense compositional network/history, not a primitive bidirectional edge or one privileged coupling operator.
- The primitive search should therefore avoid silently reintroducing binary objects A/B or a single coupling function merely because the diagram is convenient.

## Candidate architecture shift

Instead of:

`A --F--> B` plus `B --G--> A`, then `C = Couple(F,G)`

consider:

`{m_i}` = many local, directed, time-indexed mappings / transformations / interaction events

with partial composition relations such as:

`m_j o m_i` when compatible,

and higher-order materializations at coarser scales:

`micro mappings -> composite mappings -> recurrent patterns -> apparent participants/boundaries/objects/protocols/personas`.

The network need not be reducible to one total sequential chain. Branching, convergence, concurrency, asynchronous delays, and overlapping participation remain open requirements.

## Critical distinction

`BINARY_COUPLING_AS_PRIMITIVE` != `RECIPROCALITY_AS_EMERGENT_COMPOSITION_PATTERN`

The current Owner intuition is closer to the latter, but no primitive is selected.

## New primitive-search pressure test

At maximum feasible resolution, determine whether the irreducible basis is best represented as:

1. local directed mapping events,
2. typed morphisms / source-mapping-target triples,
3. composition/compatibility relations among mappings,
4. a more general interaction/process primitive from which directionality itself is derived,
5. or another formalism not yet considered.

Do not assume that 'smallest physical timeslice' equals primitive. Primitive should be tested as the minimal generative vocabulary whose further decomposition would lose required semantics or explanatory power.
