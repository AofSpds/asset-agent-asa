## Owner intent
The Owner wants the research/architecture committee to independently bring forward serious formal candidates in which Object need not be primitive and Mapping / Function / Morphism / Transformation / Interaction can plausibly sit closer to the primitive layer.

The committee should not merely affirm the current Owner worldview. It should search independently, attack the hypothesis, and bring alternatives that may be better.

## Expected outputs from committee work
For each candidate formalism/model family:
1. State the minimal primitive set and composition/interaction rules.
2. Identify hidden Object-like assumptions (domain/codomain, identity, type, carrier, interface, state space, node, rule table, etc.).
3. Show whether Object/Identity/Boundary can be recovered or materialized rather than assumed.
4. Show the smallest worked or executable example.
5. Test whether mappings/mechanisms themselves may change: f_t -> f_(t+1).
6. Identify any meta-rule regress or fixed-rule displacement problem.
7. Test branching, differentiation, merge, re-separation, and multiscale bundle materialization.
8. Give strongest counterarguments and failure conditions.
9. Compare against at least one strong Object-first control model.
10. Distinguish mathematical possibility from suitability as AAA World Model substrate.

## Research posture
Desired posture: FIGHT UNTIL CONVERGENCE IS EARNED.
Do not optimize for agreement with Owner. Optimize for finding the strongest formalization, including alternative worldviews that survive the same pressure.

## Current central question
Can a world model begin from mappings / transformations / interactions and recover useful Object-like structure without smuggling the Object back in as a hidden primitive?
