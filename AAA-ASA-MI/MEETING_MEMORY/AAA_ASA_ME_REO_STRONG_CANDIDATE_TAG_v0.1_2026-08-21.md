## 1. Owner-explicit worldview clarification

OWNER_EXPLICIT:

- The motivation for keeping the model non-rigid is not merely an engineering convenience.
- The Owner currently hypothesizes that reality itself operates in a highly relational, interaction-driven, dynamically reconfigurable way.
- The present diversity of interactions in the world is viewed as a consequence of that underlying relational and non-rigid character.
- Therefore flexibility and low premature structural commitment are world-model fidelity requirements, not only software extensibility requirements.

## 2. Strong candidate tag

TAG =
`STRONG_CANDIDATE / HIGH_WORLDVIEW_FIT / HIGH_INTERACTION_FIT / NOT_SELECTED`

SUBJECT =
Reo / Coordination Model family, especially its first-class interaction/connector semantics, composability, heterogeneous communication semantics, and dynamic reconfiguration concepts.

This tag does NOT mean:
- Reo is the canonical world model;
- Reo is Owner Accepted;
- Reo's black-box component assumptions are accepted;
- Protocol == Relation/Event has been established;
- Reo solves rich Persona/world semantics;
- mutation semantics have been resolved.

## 3. Why the tag was raised

High-fit aspects:

1. Interaction is modeled as a first-class structure rather than merely as an internal behavior of objects.
2. Interaction structure can be composed without requiring every object/component to internalize all interaction logic.
3. Different interaction/communication semantics can coexist.
4. Interaction structures can be reconfigured, including work on behavior-preserving reconfiguration.
5. This supports low premature commitment and high adaptability while retaining explicit formal constraints.
6. The architecture is compatible with the Owner's hypothesis that the diversity of observable interactions arises because the world itself is not rigidly organized around fixed object-local rules.

## 4. Critical unresolved gap

The largest known gap remains:

`REO-LIKE LOW-RIGIDITY INTERACTION STRUCTURE`
+
`RICH WORLD / OBJECT / PERSONA / HISTORY / PERSPECTIVE / RELATION / EVENT SEMANTICS`

Reo traditionally treats components largely as black boxes. AAA-ASA-ME requires investigation of models where interaction may also constitute or transform Object state, Relation, Identity, Meaning, or subsequent interaction possibilities.

## 5. Research handling

Preserve competing hypotheses.

Do NOT eliminate:
- commitment/semantic-relation models;
- process/event-first models;
- interaction-constitutive models;
- architectures where Protocol is separate from Relation/Event;
- architectures where Protocol emerges from deeper interaction semantics;
- non-Reo alternatives with better explanatory power.

Use Reo as a high-priority prior-art seed and comparison anchor, not as a winner.

## 6. Next research question

Primary next question:

`Can a Reo-like first-class, composable, reconfigurable interaction structure be extended or coupled with a rich world/object semantics without reintroducing the rigidity that the architecture was intended to avoid?`

Secondary question:

`Are there existing formalisms in which interaction not only coordinates black-box components but also constitutively changes participant state, relation, identity, meaning, or future interaction topology?`
