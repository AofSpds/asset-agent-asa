## Owner clarification
- The attraction of opposite-direction mapping bundles is not merely a macro-level reciprocal relation; it is motivated by time and the intuition that 1ms-ago self no longer exists as the current self.
- A static bidirectional edge may therefore be a low-resolution abstraction over future-directed mappings between time-local states/events.
- However, if real-world interactions are always coupled bundles and cannot be meaningfully separated without losing the interaction, the coupled opposite-direction bundle itself may be a primitive candidate.
- Owner explicitly questions whether primitive analysis should use the highest available resolution rather than a low-resolution abstraction.
- Current uncertainty remains open: single directed mapping primitive vs coupled opposite-direction mapping-bundle primitive vs joint interaction primitive.

## Key discrimination principle candidate
Primitive is not automatically the smallest temporal slice. A candidate is more primitive if decomposition into smaller pieces loses generative/semantic information that cannot be recovered without adding an extra coupling/constraint rule.

## Next interview target
Determine whether a coupled mapping bundle is reducible to individual future-directed mappings plus an independently specified coupling rule, or whether the bundle/coupling is irreducible and constitutive of the mappings, participants, or their source/target semantics.
