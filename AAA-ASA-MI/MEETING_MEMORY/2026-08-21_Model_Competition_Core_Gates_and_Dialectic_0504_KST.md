## 0. Owner clarification

Owner compressed the intended model-competition doctrine into four operational expectations:

1. MODEL VALIDATION IS REQUIRED.
2. A candidate must pass basic World Model qualification.
3. The candidate must deeply reference the current ASA-MI worldview and produce a World Model aligned with the project purpose.
4. The candidate must explain and defend the model; it must win through logic, not merely by matching the current worldview or by rhetorical force.

This record preserves that clarification as working research intent.

## 1. Basic qualification precedes worldview competition

`BASIC_QUALIFICATION != WORLDVIEW_ALIGNMENT`.

A candidate must first be a substantive World Model rather than only:
- rhetoric,
- ontology vocabulary,
- a ledger,
- a query layer,
- a runtime architecture,
- an epistemic wrapper,
- or a generic metalanguage with no native consequences.

Expected basic evidence includes, as applicable:
- explicit world semantics,
- visible commitments/assumptions,
- native consequences,
- bounded operationalization or microprobe,
- falsification/revision surface,
- history/change treatment,
- non-trivial computational or formal contact.

Material model semantic/design work remains subject to attached AAA-MODEL-DESIGN-VALIDATOR review according to project validation architecture. Qualification/admission is not Independent Validation PASS or Owner Acceptance.

## 2. Worldview comprehension and purpose alignment

After basic qualification, the challenger must use the current ASA-MI worldview as the operative INIT basis.

The task is not to imitate the Owner's current answer.
The task is to understand:
- what the project is trying to build,
- why the current hypotheses exist,
- what problems they solve,
- what tensions/open questions remain,
- and why a low-level World Model is being sought as a vessel/substrate for a human-compatible Persona.

The challenger may PRESERVE, DERIVE, REFORMULATE, CHALLENGE, or SUPERSEDE current hypotheses, subject to the current P0 boundary.

`REVISABLE != IGNORABLE`.
`UNDERSTAND BEFORE BREAKING`.

## 3. Logic / dialectic burden

A candidate must not win because:
- it resembles the Owner's worldview,
- it is novel,
- it uses sophisticated mathematics,
- it produces polished prose,
- or it receives a high scalar score alone.

It must explain why its model better serves the stated purpose and survive counterargument.

Expected dialectic sequence:

CURRENT_WORLDVIEW_COMPREHENSION
→ MODEL_THESIS
→ PURPOSE_ALIGNMENT_CASE
→ CURRENT_HYPOTHESIS_PRESERVATION_OR_BREAK_MAP
→ MODEL-SPEC / EVIDENCE CONTACT
→ ASA COUNTERARGUMENT
→ CHALLENGER REBUTTAL OR REVISION
→ FINAL ADVOCACY / WITHDRAWAL

A challenger may legitimately conclude:
- keep the current hypothesis,
- revise/narrow/split it,
- or replace it.

If replacement is advocated, the challenger must account for:
- explanatory or computational gain,
- lost value,
- semantic/design/implementation switching cost,
- new assumptions and risks,
- and why the net gain still justifies change.

## 4. Meaning of "win through logic"

`LOGICAL_WIN != RHETORICAL_WIN`.

Working interpretation:
- internal coherence matters,
- premises and commitments must be visible,
- conclusions should follow from the proposed semantics or evidence,
- counterexamples must be answered rather than ignored,
- model claims should make discriminating predictions/consequences where possible,
- hidden ontology relocation or imported bridges count against the argument,
- and a strong challenger should abandon its own model when the evidence no longer supports it.

The final research products remain broader than a winner:
- MODEL SET,
- THEORY SET,
- WORLDVIEW DELTA SET,
- detected gaps,
- defended current hypotheses,
- and justified replacements.
