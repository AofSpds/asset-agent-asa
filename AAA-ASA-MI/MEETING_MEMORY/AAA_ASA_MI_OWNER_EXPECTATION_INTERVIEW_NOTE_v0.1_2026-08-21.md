## 2. Capture without expansion

- The Owner wants both diverse models that represent the current working worldview well and strong alternative-worldview models that may diverge materially from it.
- The Owner hopes at least one candidate is personally compelling enough to merit deeper examination.
- Persuasiveness matters to the Owner, but the Owner explicitly recognizes that persuasiveness is subjective and that AI-generated persuasiveness is variable: sometimes strikingly strong, sometimes weak.
- Because the research ultimately serves an Owner-defined human purpose, the Owner believes richer interviewing of the Owner may improve the quality of later interpretation and evaluation.

## 3. Interpretation restrictions

- Do not equate Owner persuasiveness with scientific truth, model validity, or methodological PASS.
- Do not dismiss Owner persuasiveness as mere taste; it can be a separate human-purpose observation surface.
- Do not assume that more interviews are automatically better. Additional interviews may create anchoring, demand characteristics, vocabulary lock-in, or overfitting to the Owner's present articulation if poorly designed.
- Do not convert interview answers directly into universal ontology requirements.
- Preserve exact Owner statements separately from ASA/researcher interpretation.
- Do not expose candidate identities, Track labels, AI evaluator scores, or prior rankings during any later blind Owner candidate review unless the review phase explicitly requires reveal.

## 4. Recommended interview design for this research program

Use interviews to increase signal while controlling contamination.

PRE-RESULT INTERVIEW:
- purpose and desired qualities,
- current uncertainties,
- what kinds of discovery would be useful,
- what kinds of evidence would be untrustworthy,
- what must remain open,
- what human consequences matter.

BLIND POST-RESULT INTERVIEW:
- show candidate explanations without Track labels, rankings, evaluator scores, or model provenance,
- ask what is understandable, compelling, surprising, weak, or missing,
- ask what concrete consequences for Persona design follow,
- record first reaction before adversarial discussion.

CHALLENGE INTERVIEW:
- present strongest objections/counterexamples to candidates the Owner initially likes,
- ask whether preference survives,
- separate preference change caused by evidence from change caused by presentation style.

REVEAL / META-INTERVIEW:
- only after blind review, reveal Track/evaluator information,
- compare Owner preference with AI evaluation,
- investigate disagreements rather than forcing convergence.

## 5. Research interpretation

The useful principle is not simply MORE_INTERVIEW = BETTER.
A stronger formulation is:

MORE_INDEPENDENTLY_STRUCTURED_OWNER_SIGNAL + PROVENANCE + BLINDING + CONTRADICTION_CHECKS
can improve the human-purpose evaluation surface while reducing the risk that the research merely overfits to the Owner's current vocabulary.

This note is non-normative research preparation. It does not freeze an acceptance criterion or authorize model adoption.
