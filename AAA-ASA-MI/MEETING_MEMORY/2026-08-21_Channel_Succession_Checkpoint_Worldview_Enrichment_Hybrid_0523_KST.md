## 1. WHY THIS RESEARCH EXISTS

The original motivation is not to invent a fashionable World Model for its own sake.

The Owner started from the desire to create a sufficiently deep "human shell / vessel" that can contain a human-compatible AI Persona.

The low-level World Model is intended to provide a philosophical and computational substrate capable of carrying human-like existential complexity such as memory, relation, change, continuity/succession, standpoint/perspective, uncertainty, historical reinterpretation, and other dimensions that may prove necessary.

The desired abstraction does not need to literally copy human ontology. It must be capable of containing or explaining the relevant complexity while remaining computationally expressible and useful for future Persona INIT.

Philosophy is therefore not decorative. It is treated as a long-running human attempt to abstract reality. Mathematics, science, systems theory, and computer science provide additional abstraction candidates and computational forms.

Working upper-purpose statement:

`REALITY / HUMAN-EXPERIENCED REALITY -> THEORY / ABSTRACTION -> WORLD MODEL -> COMPUTATIONAL CONTACT -> PERSONA INIT SUBSTRATE`

## 2. WHAT "CURRENT HYPOTHESIS" MEANS

A major clarification occurred in this channel.

Many items called `current hypotheses` were intentionally named hypotheses because the Owner wanted them revisable and falsifiable.

In a conventional project, many would function like requirements or strong design principles.

Correct interpretation:

- current hypotheses are the CURRENT OPERATIVE / ACTIVE INIT BASIS,
- they are the best present working basis accumulated through substantial research,
- they must be deeply understood and addressed,
- they are not final truth,
- except for P0 in the current cohort, they may be preserved, derived, reformulated, narrowed, split, challenged, or superseded,
- supersession is justified only when the alternative is materially better enough to justify semantic/design/implementation switching cost and lost value.

Key rules:

`REPLACEABLE != OPTIONAL`
`REVISABLE != IGNORABLE`
`UNDERSTAND BEFORE BREAKING`

A researcher may say:
- keep this current hypothesis,
- strengthen it,
- refine it,
- replace it,
- or "change it; my alternative better serves the purpose."

But silent disregard is not an acceptable response when the current basis was supplied.

## 10. HYBRID RESEARCH ARCHITECTURE — OWNER EXPLICITLY LIKED THIS

The Owner explicitly said the following hybrid recommendation was good and should be retained:

### Whole-Model Challenger/Competition
Use whole World Models as integration pressure tests on the coherence and computational viability of a worldview/theory package.

### Modular Theory Challenge
Extract and compete theories independently in domains such as:
- identity/continuity,
- relation/constitution,
- change/ontogenesis,
- time/history,
- perspective/context,
- individuation/composition,
- human familiarity,
- sovereignty,
- and any newly discovered domains.

Purpose: harvest deep theories even from losing whole models and avoid hiding strong local contributions inside weak integrated candidates.

### Problem-First Control
Present demanding human/Persona/world problems without overexposing the current conceptual vocabulary, ask what minimum theory is required, then compare induced theories with the current Theory Set.

Purpose: detect anchoring and worldview-induced blind spots.

Risk: fixture design itself can become hidden ontology; therefore treat as control, not uncontested ground truth.

### Evolutionary Successor Lab
After initial models are frozen, select informative parents and run repeated:

`ATTACK -> FAILURE -> SUCCESSOR -> REATTACK -> MUTATION / THEORY REVISION`

Purpose: study evolvability, repairability, and whether a model can improve without collapsing into patchwork.

### Across all four

`ESSAY / DIALECTIC + MODEL SPEC / EVIDENCE`

Neither replaces the other.

This hybrid is currently the preferred architecture because it combines:
- whole-model coherence pressure,
- deep local theory harvesting,
- anti-anchoring control,
- evolutionary depth.
