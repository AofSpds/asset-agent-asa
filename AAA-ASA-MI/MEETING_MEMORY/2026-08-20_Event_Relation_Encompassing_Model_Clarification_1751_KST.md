## 3. Why Encompassing Model Is Stronger Than Identity Claim

An identity claim risks destroying distinctions that may be operationally valuable:

- persistence vs transition;
- structure vs change;
- historical evidence vs current interpretation;
- event ordering vs relation topology;
- durative process vs bounded event;
- semantic relation vs event provenance.

An encompassing model can preserve these distinctions while still explaining why EVENT and RELATION appear mutually dependent and difficult to order as first/second.

## 4. Candidate Formulations

### B. TYPE-FAMILY MODEL

### C. DYNAMICAL-CONSTITUTION MODEL
A system is modeled as evolving configurations; relations describe current constitution while events/processes describe transformations between or within configurations.

### D. HYBRID MATERIALIZATION MODEL
Persistent history/evidence remains in memory/provenance; current relations and events are separately materialized views over a shared substrate.
