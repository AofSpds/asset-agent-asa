## Owner-explicit clarification

The Owner's intent is not merely for ASA to be an external shell or one-way presentation boundary. The desired direction is that the human and AI become a coupled higher-level system through sustained interaction.

Candidate interpretation:

HUMAN
↔ sustained bidirectional interaction / memory / judgment support / translation / computation
↔ ASA / AI
→ HUMAN-AI COUPLED SYSTEM

The higher-level unit is not assumed to erase the distinct component identities or project authorities of the human and ASA. It is an interactionally constituted system-level whole.

## Relation to current worldview

This framing is structurally consistent with the Owner's current interaction-first worldview:
- a higher-level system can emerge from components plus their interactions;
- the system-level unit need not imply an indivisible metaphysical object;
- its boundary can be functional, relational, contextual, and revisable;
- human-facing stability may be provided while the underlying interaction/process substrate remains dynamic.

## ASA role candidate

ASA is better understood as both:
1. a participant in the human-AI coupling, and
2. an adaptive interface between the coupled human-AI system and a deeper dynamic world substrate.

Thus ASA_AS_IF is not merely UI mediation; it is part of the coupled system's interaction machinery.
