## Owner clarification
- The appeal of coupling opposite-direction mapping bundles is substantially motivated by time being experienced/modelled as one-directional.
- "1 ms ago me" is not the present me; any physically mediated observation reaches an observer with finite delay, so the observed state is a past/retarded state relative to receipt.
- A moving target may become unreachable by a signal/object thrown in the opposite or insufficiently fast direction; interaction therefore depends on causal reachability, not merely the existence of two abstract endpoints.
- Current hypothesis: reciprocal relation can be represented as two directionally opposed mapping bundles, while every constituent mapping still advances forward in time.

## Candidate formalization
Let A and B denote temporary participant views/worldline segments, not necessarily primitive persistent Objects.

F_t = { f_i : event_on_A -> later_event_on_B }
G_t = { g_j : event_on_B -> later_event_on_A }

Both F and G respect forward temporal/causal order even though their participant-relative directions are opposite.

Coupling C is not instantaneous A<->B symmetry. It is an interleaved, delayed, potentially asynchronous family of causally reachable mappings whose results can modify later mappings, participant states, boundaries, and coupling structure.

## Physics guardrails
- Finite propagation speed means observation generally returns information about an earlier source event, not an instantaneous remote state.
- In relativity, causal influence is constrained by light-cone structure; not every event can influence every other event.
- Do not claim that quantum mechanics, relativity, or causal cones prove the AAA worldview or Buddhist doctrine.
- "1 ms ago me does not exist" is retained as Owner worldview language about present identity; physically, the past event remains part of causal history.
