## Why it is tagged strongly
- Distinguishes the system from any privileged internal representation.
- Treats a system in terms of allowed behavior/trajectories rather than one canonical internal mechanism.
- Interconnection changes the joint behavior space rather than merely attaching a passive property.
- Supports hierarchical tearing/zooming/linking: a composed whole can become a module at a higher scale.
- Complex interconnection can itself be represented as a module/system, resonating with the Owner's interaction-as-first-class-system intuition.

## Critical gap
The classical framework generally starts with systems and interconnects them. The Owner's stronger hypothesis permits interaction to reshape or partly constitute the participating systems themselves. Therefore Willems is a strong prior-art seed, not an exact match.
