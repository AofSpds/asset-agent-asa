## 2. Strong Current Worldview Candidate

Working formulation:

> `RELATIONAL CONSTITUTION`: what an instance is may be constituted substantially by the relations and relational configuration in which it participates. Relation is therefore not merely an edge added after an independently complete essence has already been specified; relation/configuration may be closer to the practical essence itself.

Important qualification:

`ESSENCE` here MUST NOT be silently converted into an immutable metaphysical substance.

Under ASA-MI P0, the phrase currently means something closer to:

- constitutive explanatory basis;
- what makes the current instance materially what it is in the current model;
- current relational constitution;
- a strong worldview hypothesis subject to successor revision.

It does NOT mean an eternal fixed essence.

---

## 3. Consequences if the Strong Form Is Retained

Possible downstream implications to research:

1. participant roles may arise within relations;
2. membership may itself be a relation;
3. boundary may be a relation, relation-sensitive operator, or materialized consequence of relations;
4. self may be a relational configuration/process-bundle attribution rather than a primitive identity substance;
5. succession may be better than continuity because each relational configuration can materialize a distinct instance;
6. merge/fission/recomposition are natural changes of relational constitution;
7. each useful scale may materialize different instances without selecting one final ontology;
8. relation-to-relation and higher-order relational structures may be necessary in some domains;
9. a relationless entity may become semantically indeterminate, operationally non-materializable, or — in the strongest unresolved reading — nonexistent.

---

## 4. Critical Distinction — Strong Thesis vs Implementation

Even if semantic relational constitution is retained:

`RELATIONAL CONSTITUTION != UNIVERSAL GRAPH IMPLEMENTATION`

A bounded relation bundle may be represented by the cheapest adequate structure:

- tree;
- table;
- function/mapping;
- graph;
- hypergraph;
- event projection;
- rule/constraint system;
- hybrid.

INIT selection is based on implementation suitability, not philosophical elegance.

---

## 5. Core Adversarial Target

The strong thesis should be attacked directly:

> Is relation genuinely constitutive, or are relations merely abstractions/compressions of more fundamental processes/events/entities?

Major counter-hypotheses include:

- PROCESS/EVENT-FIRST;
- ENTITY-FIRST + EVENT SOURCING;
- primitive/carrier + relational projection;
- hybrid substrate -> relation materialization -> instance materialization.

A strong thesis that cannot specify what evidence would weaken it risks becoming unfalsifiable (`everything is relation`).
