## Owner-explicit hypothesis
- The observed upper bound on information propagation speed may be interpretable, in the Owner's current World Model hypothesis, as a limit associated with the processing/propagation capacity of the most local primitive mappings.
- This is a hypothesis only, not a physics claim and not identified with Causal Set Theory.
- Current World Model direction remains: innumerable local mappings compose into larger composite mappings/networks; object, boundary, reciprocity, and coupling may be higher-resolution-derived or coarse-grained structures.

## Causal Set Theory comparator
- Causal Set Theory (CST) proposes fundamentally discrete spacetime represented by a locally finite causal partial order.
- Elements correspond, in the continuum approximation, to spacetime events; the order relation corresponds to causal precedence/reachability; counting elements corresponds to spacetime volume (the slogan "order + number ~ geometry").
- CST does not, at its kinematic core, encode a per-element compute throughput or a local function-processing latency. Its primitive content is closer to event + causal order + local finiteness than to transformation semantics.
- Therefore the Owner's local-mapping-processing-limit hypothesis is distinct and potentially richer: it asks whether propagation constraints could arise from the finite dynamics of primitive local transformations themselves.

## Important adversarial point
- CST's combination of fundamental discreteness and Lorentz invariance produces a characteristic non-locality. Therefore "fundamental discreteness" does NOT automatically imply a regular local nearest-neighbour lattice or finite-valence primitive network.
- This is an important challenge to any AAA World Model hypothesis that assumes primitive mappings are local in an ordinary spatial-neighbour sense.

## Research discrimination questions
1. Is primitive structure only `event + causal order`, or does it require transformation content `m: e_i -> e_j`?
2. Can propagation-speed bounds be derived from causal-order geometry alone, or must primitive mappings have explicit processing/propagation constraints?
3. What definition of locality survives Lorentz invariance without imposing a preferred frame/lattice?
4. Can mapping mechanism mutation be represented in CST-like order-only primitives, or is a richer rewrite/process formalism required?
5. Can Object/Persona/history emerge as coarse-grained persistent patterns over the causal/mapping network without primitive Object identity?

## Control note
Do not claim that CST validates the Owner worldview, Buddhism, mapping-first ontology, or the local-mapping-processing-limit hypothesis. Use CST as a competing prior-art/formal comparator.
