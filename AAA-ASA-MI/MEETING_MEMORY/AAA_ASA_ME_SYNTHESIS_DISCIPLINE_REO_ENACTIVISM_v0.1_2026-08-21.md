## Trigger

Owner observed that Reo and Enactivism both appear harvestable and asked whether development likely requires assembling prior-art ideas first.

## Current interpretation

Do NOT treat prior-art synthesis as naive feature aggregation.

Reo and Enactivism are complementary but potentially ontologically inconsistent:
- Reo gains tractability by separating components from first-class coordination/interaction structure.
- Enactivism questions whether participant boundaries and meanings are fully pre-given, allowing interaction to constitutively alter participants and meaning.

Therefore a direct merge may destroy the strongest property of either approach.

## Recommended synthesis discipline

1. Harvest independently:
   - primitive / invariant / capability / limitation / implementation contact from each lineage.
2. Preserve competing seeds before synthesis.
3. Generate explicit successor hypotheses rather than editing the source models in place.
4. Test small discriminating microprobes before system-scale implementation.
5. Only develop a larger architecture after one or more successor hypotheses survive contradiction, tractability, and flexibility tests.

## Candidate successor directions

A. REO_PLUS_ENDOGENOUS_PARTICIPANT_CHANGE
- Reo-like explicit interaction/coordination semantics.
- Participants expose richer state and may change due to interaction.
- Risk: component/coordination separation may become incoherent.

B. ENACTIVE_SUBSTRATE_PLUS_REO_PROJECTION
- Coupled process/interaction dynamics are primary.
- Reo-like connectors are local analyzable/materialized views rather than the ontology itself.
- Risk: formal compositional guarantees may weaken.

C. PROCESS_FIRST_MULTI_VIEW_MODEL
- Objects, relations, events, and protocols are context-dependent views/materializations of a more general process/interaction substrate.
- Risk: universal-meta-framework / low falsifiability.

D. KEEP_SEPARATE_ECOLOGY
- Do not synthesize; use different model families for different scopes and coordinate at a higher layer.
- Risk: cross-model semantic translation and continuity burden.

## Development implication

The likely path is not `assemble everything -> implement once`.
It is closer to:

`PRIOR ART HARVEST`
→ `ORTHOGONAL CAPABILITY MAP`
→ `COMPETING SUCCESSOR HYPOTHESES`
→ `MICROPROBES`
→ `MINIMAL EXECUTABLE CORE(S)`
→ `FAILURE / LIMIT ANALYSIS`
→ `THEN broader development`

Synthesis is justified only if it creates a model that can explain and support interactions neither source model handles cleanly, without becoming unconstrained.
