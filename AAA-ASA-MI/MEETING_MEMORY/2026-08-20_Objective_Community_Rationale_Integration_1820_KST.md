## 2. Source-Backed Existing Whitepaper Anchors

Existing PCS-SHAI working sources already contain the following relevant propositions/hypotheses:

- the central project question concerns preserving human-side governance under deep AI integration;
- cognitive concentration may create correlated failure, common dependency, low replaceability/portability and reduction of alternative paths;
- H0/H1/H2-style survivability logic is explicitly framed for `Person/Community`, not only isolated individuals;
- Cognitive Plurality and Error Diversity are treated as possible defenses against common-mode cognitive failure;
- a dominant INIT / Protocol may itself create a monoculture and become a community-level common-mode risk;
- Community governance remains community-defined; sovereignty is not equated with one mandated political topology;
- excessive optionality/plurality can also create coordination failure, fragmentation and governance burden, so the community benefit remains a hypothesis to test rather than an axiom.

This addendum therefore does NOT create a new founding claim from nothing. It promotes an already source-backed rationale into the ASA-MI OBJECTIVE candidate map.

## 3. OBJECTIVE Purpose Candidate — Dual Rationale

Current OBJECTIVE candidate should be understood as having at least two separable rationale layers:

### A. PERSON-SIDE RATIONALE

Use AI to expand cognitive/operational capability while preserving human-side governance over identity, subjectivity, memory, judgment criteria, authority, history, exit, replacement and reconfiguration.

### B. COMMUNITY-SIDE RATIONALE

Preserving viable cognitive/governance plurality, replaceability, exit/reconfiguration paths and non-common-mode dependency at the Person/Community level may improve collective survivability, resilience, adaptability and recovery under uncertainty.

Important:

`COMMUNITY BENEFIT != PROVEN`

and

`PERSON SOVEREIGNTY != ALWAYS COMMUNITY OPTIMUM`.

Possible conflict between individual optionality and community coordination remains an explicit counter-hypothesis/research target.

## 4. Working Objective Synthesis

A current working synthesis is:

> Expand human cognitive and operational capability through deep AI integration while preserving human-side governance sovereignty; investigate whether preserving heterogeneous viable paths, replaceability, recovery and reconfiguration at Person/Community scales also improves collective survivability and resilience under uncertainty.

This wording is a working ASA-MI objective synthesis only.

It is NOT a canonical/frozen Whitepaper sentence and must not be back-projected into historical artifacts as if already adopted there.

## 5. Why This Matters for ASA-MI

ASA-MI is not only studying how an individual Persona/Self instance changes. If the project later models Community instances, the same core research questions can be evaluated across scale:

- what is materialized as a community member;
- how cognitive paths remain plural or collapse;
- what common-mode dependency emerges;
- how exit/reconfiguration works;
- whether sovereignty mechanisms increase or decrease coordination cost;
- whether a successful protocol itself becomes a new monoculture root.

This is compatible with the current multi-scale materialization hypothesis without making Community the current discussion topic.
