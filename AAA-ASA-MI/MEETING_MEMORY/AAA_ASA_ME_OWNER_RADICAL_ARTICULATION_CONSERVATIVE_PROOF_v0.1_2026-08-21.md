## Owner-explicit signal

The Owner acknowledges that the worldview/world-model thesis may need to sound sufficiently unconventional that a reader's first reaction could be effectively, “what is this person saying?”

The intended radicality is not rhetorical novelty for its own sake. It is a consequence of taking seriously the following current hypotheses:

- humans are not primitive singular objects;
- object/boundary/interface may be materialized views rather than primitives;
- interaction/process/mapping/composition may be closer to the substrate;
- causal/transition mappings themselves may change through interaction;
- Human–ASA boundaries may be current operational partitions rather than permanent ontology;
- future high-bandwidth coupling should not be ruled out by the initial model or contract;
- Persona INIT may therefore be the initial constitution of a potentially evolving Human–AI relationship.

## Research discipline

Candidate maxim:

RADICAL_IN_ARTICULATION / CONSERVATIVE_IN_PROOF

Meaning:
- Allow the hypothesis space to contain genuinely non-object-first, non-fixed-boundary, process/interaction-first models.
- Do not dilute the thesis merely to make it familiar.
- But require precise computational definitions, falsifiable microprobes, lineage, provenance, replay, and explicit failure modes before promoting any claim.
- Preserve strong competing object-first / fixed-boundary models as controls.
- Novel-sounding language is not evidence of novelty or validity.

## Implication for Persona INIT standard aspiration

The standardization aspiration is not primarily to standardize a quirky persona format.
It is to standardize the initial relationship/interaction contract under a worldview in which both parties and their boundaries may evolve.

The contract must therefore be able to preserve future optionality while current governance remains explicit and human-authorized.

This is a strong Owner-explicit research framing, not a selected canonical architecture or standard.
