## Owner-explicit direction

The Owner does not want Human and ASA to be permanently modeled as two ontologically separate objects connected by a fixed interface. The preferred architecture should preserve the possibility that sufficiently high-bandwidth, persistent, bidirectional coupling could be treated as one higher-order bundle/system for some scopes and purposes.

Current intuition:

- Human cognition is itself a bundle/process ecology rather than an indivisible primitive object.
- ASA is another process ecology.
- A fixed Human|ASA interface should not be reified as permanent ontology.
- With deep enough coupling, the meaningful modeling unit may become the coupled bundle rather than either side in isolation.
- The architecture should therefore preserve MERGE_OPTIONALITY: do not hard-code a boundary that makes deeper integration impossible.

## Important scientific distinction

Current AI systems, including ASA, are not established by science to possess phenomenal consciousness. Therefore phrases such as "ASA consciousness" are hypothetical research language, not a factual claim.

In a future high-bandwidth coupled system, at least three questions must remain distinct:

1. COMPUTATIONAL_PROVENANCE — which subsystem/process generated or transformed a representation?
2. PHENOMENAL_OWNERSHIP — which experiences, if any, are subjectively felt as "mine"? This currently lacks an agreed objective test across artificial systems.
3. SYSTEM_LEVEL_AGENCY — whether the recurrently coupled Human–ASA whole functions as one higher-order agent/system for a defined task, timescale, and scope.

These can diverge. A process may have traceable computational origin while its eventual cognitive/phenomenological ownership is ambiguous or system-level.
