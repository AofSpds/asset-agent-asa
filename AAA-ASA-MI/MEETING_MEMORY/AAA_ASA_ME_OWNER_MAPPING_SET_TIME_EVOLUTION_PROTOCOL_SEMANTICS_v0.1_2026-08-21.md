## Owner clarification
- A current self/persona can be viewed as a **set/bundle of mappings** rather than one fixed object.
- The mapping-set changes over time; intuitively `M_t -> M_(t+1)` under time/history/interactions.
- An individual mapping need not be restricted to a single deterministic one-input/one-output function. Depending on mapping kind, one source process may branch to two or more successors, and multiple sources may combine.
- Therefore the cardinality/shape/semantics of interaction are not globally fixed.
- This is why the Owner has repeatedly emphasized **protocol**: protocol is intended to govern/express the admissible mapping/interaction form, composition, branching/merge behavior, and interpretation for the current context rather than merely being a fixed external API.

## Formalization caution
Strict set-theoretic functions are single-valued. The Owner's "mapping/function" intuition should therefore be tested against broader structures such as relations, multivalued maps, nondeterministic/stochastic transitions, morphisms/processes, spans/cospans, rewriting systems, operadic/polycategorical interaction shapes, etc. No formalism is selected.

## Key unresolved question
Is protocol itself:
1. external/fixed semantics over mappings,
2. a context-sensitive interpreter that selects among mapping types,
3. part of the mapping-set and therefore itself mutable/composable,
4. or an emergent regularity of interactions?
This is a critical next-step question because it determines whether rigidity merely moves from mapping to protocol/meta-rule.

## Research implication
Candidate world models should support time-evolving mapping bundles, variable interaction arity/shape, branching and merge, and should explain protocol evolution without hidden fixed-object assumptions or unexamined meta-rule rigidity.
