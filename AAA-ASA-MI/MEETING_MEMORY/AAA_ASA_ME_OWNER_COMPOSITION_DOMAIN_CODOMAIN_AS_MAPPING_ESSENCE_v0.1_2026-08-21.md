## Clarification
Owner states that composability is close to the essence of a mapping/function rather than an externally chosen contextual rule. Functions have domains and codomains, and composition follows from structural compatibility of those mappings.

Core intuition:
- Mapping/function is not merely an arbitrary relation label; it has source/target structure.
- Composition is available when the relevant source/target structure is compatible.
- A composition of mappings can be abstracted and treated as a higher-order single mapping.
- Not every generalized/multi-mapping need be composable.

## Critical open question for WM-01
If mappings are primitive candidates, what ontological status do domain/codomain have?

1. DOMAIN/CODOMAIN_AS_PRIMITIVE_OBJECTS — would reintroduce Object-like primitives.
2. DOMAIN/CODOMAIN_AS_DERIVED_COMPATIBILITY_BOUNDARIES — source/target structure is recoverable from mapping/composition structure.
3. HYBRID — some typed boundary structure is primitive while higher-level Objects remain derived.

This is a hidden-object test for Mapping-first World Model candidates.

## Formal precedent to test, not adopt
Objectless/category-style formulations suggest that object-like structure may be recoverable from identity/composition behavior, but this must not be treated as a selected solution. The committee should demonstrate whether domain/codomain can be represented without smuggling full Object primitives back in.
