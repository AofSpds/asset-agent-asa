## Owner clarification

Owner states that the repeated questions “source or target?”, “source+target+mapping?”, and “what is time relative to mapping?” feel structurally similar. The currently more attractive hypothesis is not a single directed arrow as primitive, but two bundles of directed mappings in opposite directions that are coupled.

Conceptual sketch:

- Bundle A→B: one or more directed mappings from side A toward side B.
- Bundle B→A: one or more directed mappings from side B toward side A.
- The two directional bundles are coupled and may jointly constitute the interaction.
- Either side may change through the coupled interaction.
- The coupling itself, rather than either one-way mapping alone, may be the deeper primitive candidate.
- Directionality need not be discarded; it may remain intrinsic to each mapping while the interaction unit is the coupled pair/bundle.
- Source/target/object boundaries may be transient or role-relative materializations rather than eternal objects.

## Important distinction

Do not collapse this immediately into “bidirectional = two inverse functions.” The two bundles need not be inverses, symmetric, deterministic, equal-strength, or composable in the same way. They may carry different semantics, time constants, multiplicities, or update effects.

Candidate minimal form:

I_t = Coupling(F_t^{A→B}, G_t^{B→A})

where F and G can themselves be sets/bundles of mappings and may evolve over time.

Possible state evolution:

(A_t, B_t, F_t, G_t, C_t) → (A_{t+1}, B_{t+1}, F_{t+1}, G_{t+1}, C_{t+1})

This is only a sketch; it is not a canonical model.
