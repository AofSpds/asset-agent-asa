## Owner Direction
Legacy artifacts and historical concepts remain preserved as historical records. When a currentized ASA-MI hypothesis/objective/concept is meaningfully connected to a legacy concept, add an explicit lineage/link rather than rewriting the legacy source into current meaning.

## Working Rule

`LEGACY != CURRENT`

`LEGACY_SOURCE_PRESERVED = TRUE`

`CURRENTIZATION_MAY_LINK_TO_LEGACY = TRUE`

`LINK != RETROACTIVE_REWRITE`

A lineage link may indicate, as appropriate:
- ancestor / conceptual precursor;
- refined or strengthened successor;
- partial preservation;
- split into multiple current hypotheses;
- current reinterpretation under a different ontology/model;
- conflict or rejected branch that remains historically informative.

The exact relation vocabulary is not yet canonical and should not be over-formalized during brainstorming.

## Example Direction

Historical `BOUNDARY = GOVERNED RELATIONAL STATUS` may remain intact in PCS-SHAI legacy while linking forward to current candidates such as:
- RELATIONAL CONSTITUTION;
- MEMBRANE / Coupling Governance Surface;
- Scope-local callability/materialization;
- Event/Relation-centered modeling.

Likewise historical Persona/Self/Memory concepts may link to current Successor Instance, Composition, Materialization, and Memory Layer hypotheses without claiming they meant exactly the same thing at the time.

## Re-harvest Principle

Whitepaper re-harvest should therefore produce both:
1. candidate material for currentization; and
2. explicit lineage where a historical idea materially contributed to a current candidate.

Historical artifacts are not rewritten to make them appear more current than they were.
