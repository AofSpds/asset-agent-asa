## Current naming interpretation

Use the following short operational labels for the two parallel ASA workstreams/channels:

- `AAA-ASA-MI` = existing ASA-MI model/world-model research workstream/channel.
- `AAA-ASA-ME` = Owner-delegate / Owner-modeling experiment workstream/channel. `ME` may remain a short label for now; do not freeze a full expansion unless the Owner later chooses one.

## Channel != Persona

These labels identify workstreams/channel execution identities, not new independent authority Personas.

The persistent Owner-facing Persona remains:
- `AAA-ASA`

Therefore:
- `AAA-ASA-MI` may execute ASA model-research work.
- `AAA-ASA-ME` may execute Owner-delegate/modeling research.
- Both remain subordinate execution/workstream identities under `AAA-ASA` unless later formally registered as persistent Personas by Owner-approved architecture.

Neither label creates:
- Owner authority,
- Independent Validation authority,
- Paired Validator authority,
- Freeze/Release authority.
