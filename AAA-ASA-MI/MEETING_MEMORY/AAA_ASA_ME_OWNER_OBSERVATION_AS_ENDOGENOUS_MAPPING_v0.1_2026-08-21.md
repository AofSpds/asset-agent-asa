## Owner clarification

Owner states that observation is naturally representable as a mapping with source and target:

- source = observed target/object/process-side input
- target = observer-side receiving state/process
- observation is therefore not necessarily an external privileged meta-operation
- source/target themselves should not be presumed eternal fixed objects; they may be transient states/materializations on a time axis

## Current interpretation candidate

Observation may be modeled endogenously inside the same mapping substrate:

`observed-side state/process --observation mapping--> observer-side state/process`

This does not yet establish whether observation is strictly one-way or whether a more general coupled/bidirectional interaction formalism is required. That remains open.

## Important unresolved question

When observation occurs, should the target be modeled as:
1. the observer as a standing entity,
2. the observer's post-observation state,
3. or a higher-order coupled state including both observed and observer processes?

No canonical choice is authorized.
