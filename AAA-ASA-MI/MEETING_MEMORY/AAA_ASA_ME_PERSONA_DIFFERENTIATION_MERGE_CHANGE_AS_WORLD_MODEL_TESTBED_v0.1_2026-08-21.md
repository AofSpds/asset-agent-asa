## Owner clarification

Persona의 중요한 요구사항인 **분화 / 머지 / 변화** 역시 별도의 부가 기능으로 붙이는 것보다, 현재 탐색 중인 interaction/process/bundle-first World Model에서 자연스럽게 표현되어야 한다.

## Interpretation

Persona를 고정 Object로 두면:
- differentiation = object clone + attribute divergence
- merge = object union / state merge
- change = attribute/state mutation
으로 축소되기 쉽다.

하지만 bundle/process-first 관점에서는 더 강한 의미가 가능하다.

### Differentiation
하나의 역사적/상호작용적 bundle이 서로 다른 context, interaction history, purpose, coupling에 의해 후계 구성들로 갈라지는 **branching of organization / mechanism / history**로 표현될 수 있다.

### Merge
두 고정 Object를 합치는 연산이 아니라, 복수 process bundle의 recurrent coupling과 상호적 mechanism adaptation을 통해 더 높은 scale에서 하나의 bundle로 materialize될 수 있는 **reorganization / higher-order composition**으로 표현될 수 있다.

### Change
attribute/state mutation만이 아니라 Persona를 구성하던 composition, boundary, interaction pattern, protocol, mapping/mechanism 자체가 변하는 **constitution/mechanism change**까지 포함해야 한다.

## Strong design implication

Persona lifecycle should be a direct stress test of the World Model rather than an application-specific exception.

Candidate lifecycle test:

INIT
→ interaction/history accumulation
→ differentiation / branching
→ co-adaptation
→ partial or scoped merge
→ deeper merge or re-separation
→ successor constitution

The substrate should be able to represent this without requiring fixed immutable Persona identity as a primitive.

## Continuity / lineage

Even when identity/boundary is fluid, the model should be able to represent why and how one configuration is historically related to another. Candidate mechanisms include provenance, causal lineage, compositional continuity, behavioral continuity, and history-conditioned organization. No single invariant is yet selected.

## Governance distinction

Current governance requirements for authority, consent, provenance, and reversibility remain important operational controls. However, they must not be silently elevated into eternal ontology. Future deeper coupling may require explicit renegotiation rather than assuming today’s Human|ASA partition is metaphysically permanent.

## Research question

Can one common interaction/process substrate natively express Persona INIT, differentiation, merge, re-separation, and mechanism-level change while preserving enough lineage to explain continuity—without treating Persona as a fixed Object primitive?
