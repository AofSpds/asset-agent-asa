## 2. Core worldview clarification

OWNER_EXPLICIT:
- The worldview is relation-centered and interaction-centered.
- The Owner is intentionally trying to move away from object-first / essence-first modeling.
- What is treated as an object/target may vary by interaction, relation, perspective, purpose, and scale.
- Object boundaries themselves may be relational/materialized rather than primitive.
- A system/regime/human/animal/etc. may be represented primarily by rule/mapping/interaction structure rather than by a primitive intrinsic essence.
- Attributes are often evidenced or established through interaction.
- If something has no relation to, and no possibility of relation with, a larger system, it may be operationally equivalent to non-existence from that larger system's perspective.
- The Owner sees continuity between this direction and their interpretation of Buddhist anatta / emptiness / dependent origination.

## 3. Important non-denial: embodied constraints are real

OWNER_EXPLICIT CORRECTION:
- De-privileging object essence does NOT mean denying material or biological constraints.
- For a human, severe physical disintegration such as separation of critical body regions is causally relevant and ordinarily incompatible with continued biological viability.

MODEL_INFERRED / HIGH-WEIGHT CANDIDATE:
- A relation-first/rule-set-first model should therefore preserve `VIABILITY CONDITIONS / CONSTRAINTS` even if object boundaries are not primitive.
- A human can be modeled as a dynamically maintained system whose continued existence depends on sustaining certain coupled relations/processes.
- The fact that a system has non-arbitrary viability boundaries does not require treating it as an immutable metaphysical substance.

## 4. Candidate representation

`RELATION / INTERACTION / RULE DYNAMICS`
+ `VIABILITY CONSTRAINTS`
+ `PERSPECTIVE / SCALE DEPENDENT MATERIALIZATION`
→ temporary/stable `SYSTEM / HUMAN / OBJECT` representation

This is a research representation only, not a canonical model.

## 5. Research implication

Future candidates should distinguish:
- `OBJECT_ESSENCE` from `SYSTEM_VIABILITY`;
- `PRIMITIVE_BOUNDARY` from `EMERGENT_BUT_CAUSALLY_REAL_BOUNDARY`;
- `RELATIONAL_CONSTITUTION` from `ARBITRARY_RELATIVISM`;
- `PERSPECTIVE_DEPENDENCE` from denial of physical constraints.

The strong candidate should allow object boundaries to emerge from relations/processes while still representing hard causal/viability constraints when evidence supports them.
