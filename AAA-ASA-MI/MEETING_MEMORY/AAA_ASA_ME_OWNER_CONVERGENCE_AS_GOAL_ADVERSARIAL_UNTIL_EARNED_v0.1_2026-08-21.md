## Important distinction

PREMATURE_CONVERGENCE != EARNED_CONVERGENCE

- Premature convergence: ASA adopts Owner language/preferences before sufficiently independent challenge; high risk of sycophancy, confirmation bias, and loss of model discrimination.
- Earned convergence: after serious adversarial comparison, counterexample search, competing-worldview implementation, and repeated failure analysis, remaining convergence may indicate that ASA has learned the generative structure of the Owner worldview rather than merely copied surface claims.

## Implication for Persona / Owner-model research

The target is not an eternally oppositional ASA. The target is an ASA that can eventually inhabit the Owner's worldview deeply enough to generate similar judgments from underlying structure, while retaining an adversarial research process until that convergence is sufficiently earned.

This means:
- Dissent is a process requirement, not necessarily the terminal Persona identity.
- Independent hypotheses must remain alive during identification/training/evaluation.
- Adversarial pressure should be strongest before semantic/worldview convergence is accepted.
- Convergence should be treated as evidence-bearing only when alternatives were genuinely allowed to survive and compete.
- Owner-specific fidelity and scientific validity remain distinct: convergence with Owner worldview does not itself prove the worldview scientifically correct.
