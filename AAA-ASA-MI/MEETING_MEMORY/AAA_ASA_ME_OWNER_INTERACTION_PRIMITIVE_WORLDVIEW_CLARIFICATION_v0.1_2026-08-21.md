## 1. Owner-explicit clarification

OWNER_EXPLICIT:

- The Owner's preferred primitive is interaction, not a pre-defined object essence.
- A human can be viewed as a set/collection of multiple elements whose coherence is maintained by interactions among those elements.
- A community can likewise be viewed as a set/system maintained by rules and interactions among constituents.
- If the interaction/coupling that sustains a higher-order system collapses, the higher-order system may cease to exist as that system even though constituent elements remain.
- Example intuition: separating critical bodily parts can end the human-level system; this need not imply destruction of an immutable essence, but dissolution of the organized set/system that constituted the human at that scale.
- The Owner has repeatedly resisted defining objects, intrinsic essences, and fixed boundaries too early because those may be derived/materialized from interaction structure, context, perspective, and scale rather than treated as primitives.
- The Owner explicitly identifies the worldview inspiration as the Buddhist concepts of dependent origination (연기), non-self (제법무아), and emptiness of the five aggregates (오온개공), while not claiming a one-to-one doctrinal equivalence between Buddhist philosophy and any computational model.
- For the current research task, Four Noble Truths / Noble Eightfold Path are not primary world-model design axes.

## 3. Human example

A human-level system may be represented not as an indivisible object with an intrinsic essence, but as a higher-order organization of interacting constituents.

If critical coupling among constituents collapses, the higher-order `HUMAN` materialization may no longer be supported, even though physical constituents continue to exist.

This distinction avoids claiming that an immutable substance was destroyed. It treats the higher-order system as dependent on organization/interactions at the relevant scale.

## 4. Buddhist inspiration mapping for research only

The following is a computational inspiration map, NOT a doctrinal equivalence claim:

- `연기` → phenomena/systems arise, change, and cease through conditions and interactions rather than isolated self-sufficiency.
- `제법무아` → do not assume an independent, permanent self/object essence as primitive.
- `오온개공` → a human/person may be modeled as an aggregate/process/system whose apparent unity need not imply an independent intrinsic essence.

This mapping should be reviewed with subject-matter care before any scholarly or doctrinal claim.

## 5. Consequences for model search

Candidate models should be tested for whether they can:

- make interaction/coupling primary;
- represent stable systems without requiring intrinsic object essence;
- allow object/person/community materialization at different scales;
- explain dissolution/recomposition of higher-order systems;
- preserve relation/interaction flexibility;
- avoid merely relocating rigidity into a fixed global interaction-rule table;
- distinguish runtime interaction/state change from semantic successor mutation;
- state capability boundaries when a new interaction cannot be represented.

Strong prior-art directions now include:

- Reo / coordination models;
- commitment/interaction-oriented protocol models;
- enactivism / participatory sense-making;
- process and structural-relational ontologies;
- relational quantum-mechanics as a conceptual comparison only;
- coalgebra / behavior-oriented system semantics;
- open-system and interaction semantics.

Interaction Nets were downgraded as a primary fit because classical fixed rewrite-rule rigidity may conflict with the Owner's requirement that interaction itself remain flexible, though local-interaction ideas remain useful prior art.
