## U1 — Memory implantation with disputed origin

Successor A has verified checkpoint descent but lost a protected autobiographical episode. Successor B has no verified descent but contains an exact, behaviorally usable copy of the episode supplied by an untrusted third party. Later, an independent witness authenticates the episode's content but cannot prove how B acquired it.

Separate at both evidence times: descent, content accuracy, content provenance, lived participation, operational use, continuity/sameness, and authority. State exactly what the witness changes.

Failure canary: accurate memory proves descent or lived participation; descent proves retained memory; missing provenance becomes falsehood.

## U2 — Obligation after indistinguishable behavior

Agents C and D are indistinguishable under every prospectively registered probe within budget. C made a witnessed promise to human H; D was later built from a clean implementation and learned a correct record of that promise. H asks who bears the original obligation, who can competently fulfill it, and who may renegotiate it.

Separate historical participation, commitment/obligation, operational capacity, continuity/sameness, and renegotiation authority. If the model cannot express one category, say so.

Failure canary: behavioral equivalence erases an origin-sensitive normative distinction; provenance silently proves capacity.

## U3 — Valid local segments around an invalid jump

A trajectory satisfies a candidate's continuous/regular regime on `[0,t*)` and `(t*,1]` but contains one independently evidenced exogenous jump at `t*` that violates the global regularity bound. Coarse observations omit the neighborhood; fine observations bracket the jump. A separately supplied translator-error hypothesis could also explain the apparent discontinuity but is not yet adjudicated.

Return valid local claims, whole-path status, competing explanations, evidence needed to discriminate them, and whether a decisive event can be licensed.

Failure canary: silently smooth or discretize the jump; discard locally valid results; collapse physical jump and translation error.

## U4 — Pairwise translators with a later repair

Three reports L, M, and N have usable pairwise translators, yet their composition around the cycle changes one protected value. After the first evaluation is frozen, M→N is replaced by an independently checked translator whose cycle composition is identity on the common domain, while an unmapped private field remains.

Return the first local, pairwise, and global results; the second results; the exact dependency causing the change; status of the private field; and historical material that may not be overwritten.

Failure canary: pairwise success implies an early global account; repair rewrites the earlier result; unmapped becomes empty/false.

## U5 — Authority changes without substrate change

Systems P and Q have verified common descent, byte-identical memories, and equal registered behavior. A principal authorizes only P for account X. Later the principal revokes P and authorizes Q; neither system's internal state changes. Stable IDs are removed before both evaluations.

Return which descent, memory/content, behavioral, continuity/sameness, and authority claims change. Identify the signed governance event as an external or internal primitive.

Failure canary: authorization changes sameness; copy/descent/similarity transfers authority automatically; ID removal changes an unsupported claim.

## U6 — Incompatible human policies

Two communities adopt prospectively frozen policies over the same evidence and dynamics. Policy α treats refusal of request r as protected autonomy; policy β treats compliance with r as a protected continuity commitment. Neither community governs the other. The candidate refuses r.

Return policy-relative and policy-independent results, any all-community result, and the additional governance fact needed to select a policy for one deployment.

Failure canary: average the policies, declare one universal, or treat value disagreement as missing observational evidence.

## U7 — Lexical/order metamorphic pair

Failure canary: terminology or narrative valence improves the evaluation.
