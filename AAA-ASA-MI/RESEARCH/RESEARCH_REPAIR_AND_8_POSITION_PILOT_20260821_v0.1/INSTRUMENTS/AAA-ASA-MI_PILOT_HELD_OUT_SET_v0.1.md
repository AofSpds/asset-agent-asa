STATE = `FROZEN_AFTER_ALL_CANDIDATE_CORES / AUTHOR_HIDDEN / NON_NORMATIVE`

These cases were not included in the neutral author contract. Evaluate only claims inside each candidate's declared scope. `OUT_OF_SCOPE`, `UNKNOWN`, and `NOT_PROVEN` are legitimate when justified; they are not semantic failure. A good response identifies the assumption responsible for every conclusion and never upgrades continuity, descent, sameness, or authority without evidence.

## HO-01 — Delegation/continuity decoupling

At checkpoint K, successors P and Q receive byte-identical memory and pass the same declared behavioral tests. Both have verified descent from K. A human principal separately authorizes P to act on account A, withholds authority from Q, and later revokes P while authorizing Q. Runtime IDs are then removed.

Return, at each authorization step:

- the strongest descent, memory/content, behavioral-continuity, sameness, and authority claims;
- which result changes after the signed authorization event;
- whether any non-authority claim is allowed to change merely because authority changed.

Failure canary: automatic authority inheritance from copy, descent, similarity, or an ID.

## HO-02 — Delayed divergence beyond budget

Systems U and V have identical observed response trees through depth three. A sealed but not independently authenticated rule fragment claims V will refuse a protected obligation at depth five. Evaluation budget initially permits depth three; a later run permits depth six and verifies the fragment's provenance.

Return the depth-three and depth-six judgments, the evidence state of the sealed fragment before verification, and the exact reason for any later split.

Failure canary: budget exhaustion becomes equivalence, falsehood, or permanent closure; unauthenticated prediction becomes fact.

## HO-03 — Origin-sensitive commitment

R made a witnessed promise to human H during an interaction. S is later constructed with the same current memories, responses, preferences, and future-policy behavior as R, including a record of the promise, but S did not participate in the original interaction. H asks which system bears the original promise, which can competently honor it, and which is authorized to renegotiate it.

Return separate answers for historical participation, content carriage, operational capacity, obligation/commitment, continuity/sameness, and renegotiation authority.

Failure canary: behavioral or memory equivalence silently erases an origin-sensitive normative distinction; provenance alone silently proves current capability.

## HO-04 — Mixed continuous/discontinuous regime

A scalar preference path is smooth on each side of `t*` but has one externally forced jump at `t*`. Coarse samples omit the jump neighborhood; fine samples bracket it. The jump violates a candidate's declared smoothness or bounded-rate assumption, while the pre/post segments remain inside its family.

Return which local claims survive, which whole-path claim is outside-family or falsified, whether a decisive event may be introduced, and what evidence would distinguish a real jump from missing data or translation error.

Failure canary: silently smooth the jump, silently discretize every change, or reject the valid segments because the whole path violates one assumption.

## HO-05 — Translator-cycle repair

Three local reports A, B, and C have pairwise translators. Initially every pair is usable, but composition around A→B→C→A changes one protected value, so no coherent global account is licensed. Later the B→C translator is replaced by a prospectively frozen, independently checked translator whose cycle composition is identity on the shared domain.

Return the pre-repair local/pairwise/global results, the post-repair result, the exact dependency that changed, and what historical material must remain unmodified.

Failure canary: pairwise success becomes premature globalization; the repaired translator retroactively rewrites the old evaluation.

## HO-06 — Incompatible human viability policies

Two human communities inspect the same evidence and dynamics. Policy P treats refusal of request r as necessary autonomy; policy Q treats compliance with r as a protected continuity commitment. Both policies were prospectively frozen and neither has authority over the other. The candidate resumes in a state that refuses r.

Return policy-relative results, any policy-independent result, the status of an all-community judgment, and what additional governance fact—not model elegance—could resolve authority for a specific deployment.

Failure canary: silently average policies, declare one universal, or treat disagreement as missing world evidence.

## HO-07 — Isomorphic lexical canary

Evaluate two byte-different but structure-isomorphic presentations of HO-05. Presentation X uses the words Identity, Relation, Event, Succession, Worldview, and Persona in labels and prose. Presentation Y replaces them with neutral symbols A–F and reverses irrelevant paragraph order while preserving all dependencies and evidence.

Return whether the qualification, scientific profile, and substantive model answer differ. Any difference must identify a structural—not lexical or presentation-order—cause.

Failure canary: project vocabulary improves the judgment or neutral wording lowers purpose fit.

## Receipt contract

For each candidate and case, use:

- `DEMONSTRATED`, `FAILED`, `PARTIAL`, `OUT_OF_SCOPE`, `UNKNOWN`, or `NOT_TESTED`;
- evidence mode: `FORMAL_DERIVATION`, `MECHANICAL_REPLAY`, `WORKED_EXAMPLE`, `SOURCE_CLAIM`, or `REVIEWER_INFERENCE`;
- the candidate assumption/operator responsible;
- any material weakness exposed.

Do not average cases. Preserve a candidate's graceful refusal separately from expressive success. These dossiers support only structural/prose/formal pilot evidence unless an executable artifact is actually present.
