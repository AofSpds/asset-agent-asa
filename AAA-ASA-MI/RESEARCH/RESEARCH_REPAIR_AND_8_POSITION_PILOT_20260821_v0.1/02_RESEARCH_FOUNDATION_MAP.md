## Immediate problem structure

1. Common implementation conveniences—persistent object, identifier, record store, current snapshot, global state/time—may pre-decide continuity rather than explain it.
2. Owner intent, historical context, researcher interpretation, derived hypothesis, evaluation result, and open question have been mixed in some prior task designs.
3. Existing six finalists have incomplete source/input provenance, so missing coverage cannot be treated as candidate failure.
4. Previous scalar/rubric regimes materially changed winners; evaluator design is part of the experiment.
5. Fluent model dossiers can be tooling, ledgers, or metalanguages without native world-level consequence.
6. A human-compatible substrate must reconcile historical integrity with future reinterpretation and successor change.

## Research questions

- What, if anything, remains continuous through interruption, memory loss, copy, divergence, merge, and semantic revision?
- When is a successor related to a predecessor without asserting identity?
- Is memory constitutive, necessary-but-insufficient, or merely one signal among relation, lineage, commitment, process, and control?
- Are relations constitutive or one useful representation among several?
- How can context/perspective plurality avoid both premature globalization and arbitrary relativism?
- How should continuous change coexist with discrete lineage/replay without privileged thresholds?
- What assumptions must a candidate expose, and which consequences arise from the model rather than the fixture?
- How can historical representations remain inspectable when rules/vocabularies change?
- Which abstractions materially improve human interpretability/control rather than only formal elegance?

## Current working assumptions

- P0: use an explicit current meaning operationally without finalizing it as eternal essence.
- Historical records retain their then-applicable semantic version; currentization is a separate, possibly partial operation.
- Unknown, undefined, disputed, alternative, impossible, and not-proven must not be collapsed.
- A World Model must faithfully contain and operationalize its own stated view; project vocabulary is not mandatory.
- A storage, UI, runtime, proof ledger, or general metalanguage is insufficient without a non-trivial native consequence.
- Current hypotheses are an active INIT basis but are revisable with demonstrated benefit and accounted switching/lost-value cost.

These are current research premises, not validated truths. P0 is constitutive inside the current cohort but revisable at the broader research-program level.

## Candidate explanations now in play

- Persistent-changing-whole accounts.
- Lineage/discrete-successor accounts.
- Purpose-relative dual views that preserve human continuance and structural succession separately.
- Relation-first, process/event-first, entity-plus-event-sourcing, and hybrid accounts.
- Constraint/admissible-history, contextual/non-global, witnessed rewrite/causal, continuous/viability, interaction/interface, generative/predictive, and derived individuation families.
- Model-portfolio or theory-ecology accounts in which no single kernel is universally sufficient.

## Alternative interpretations that must remain visible

- `Identity = Memory` is an Owner proposition under research, not a proven equivalence; sufficiency is OPEN.
- Relation-first does not imply a universal graph implementation.
- Event and relation may be different projections of a phenomenon without being identical.
- Absence in a current scope may mean non-callability or non-materialization, not global nonexistence.
- Human familiarity may aid sovereignty but is not identical to sovereignty.
- Autopoiesis/enactivism may supply research ancestors without proving AI life or consciousness.
- Convergence on contextual + causal jobs may be problem-, rubric-, context-, or model-prior-induced.

- Hidden ontology in IDs, covers, equality, matches, boundaries, schemas, sampling, and fixtures.
- Unfalsifiability through unlimited context addition or post-hoc reinterpretation.
- Answer encoding in constraints/rules or evaluator fixtures.

- Premature synthesis of incompatible theories.
