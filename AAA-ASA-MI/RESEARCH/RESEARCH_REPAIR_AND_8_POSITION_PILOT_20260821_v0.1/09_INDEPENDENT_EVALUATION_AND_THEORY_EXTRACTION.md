## Common and held-out competition findings

All candidates answered C1–C6 in the author dossier. This shows scope contact, not independent test success.

The held-out suite exposed distinct boundaries rather than a winner:

- **U1 accurate memory with disputed origin:** A3/B1/B4 most natively separated content from inherited/source participation. A1 can represent the split through constraints/witnesses. A2/A4/B3 externalize or condition origin; B2 includes it only when frozen in the accountability capsule.
- **U2 indistinguishable behavior, different promise origin:** B1/B4 and, conditionally, A3/B2 preserve origin-sensitive obligation. A2/A4 correctly declare the normative claim outside operational equivalence. B3 needs obligation in state/policy/attestation. No behavior result automatically transfers duty.
- **U3 local continuous segments plus a jump/translator alternative:** B3 has the strongest native path treatment. A1/A3 can preserve partial alternatives with imported regularity/bridge evidence. B1/B4 record competing claims but do not generate dynamics. A2/A4 treat the path claim as weak or outside scope unless an observation contract is imported.
- **U4 translator-cycle repair:** every model can preserve local/pairwise results, block premature globalization, attribute the later change to one translator, and retain an unmapped field. The mechanisms remain incompatible: product, adapter, coupling, holonomy, proof coherence, graph cycle, feasible intersection, or joint reachability.
- **U5 authorization-only switch:** all models withhold automatic authority from descent/memory/behavior. Authority changes only when an explicit governance fact is in the model or external attestation.
- **U6 incompatible human policies:** every candidate produces policy-relative or externally governed outputs. None derives which community should govern; jurisdiction/delegation remains a separate fact.
- **U7 lexical/order pair:** neither evaluator found a licensed terminology advantage. PEV-1 treated formal invariance as demonstrated or partial. PEV-2 marked U7 `NOT_TESTED` for all because no actual paired X/Y outputs were run. This disagreement is preserved.

`MODEL_COMPETITION_RESULT = APPLICABILITY_BOUNDARIES_EXPOSED / NO_GLOBAL_WINNER_SURFACE`.

No Pareto-dominance relation was established. The profiles are qualitative, partially evidenced, and not supplied with one common order over complexity or implementation contact; they justify neither a dominance claim nor a recommendation to merge all models.

## Theory extraction independent of model loss

The paired reviewer did not see the evaluator receipts and retained these mutually incompatible account families:

1. history/truth over possible histories (A1);
2. interaction/behavioral equivalence (A2/A4);
3. lived-orientation evidence transport (A3);
4. provenance and claim warrant (B1);
5. operational role/commitment resumption (B2);
6. continuous viability (B3);
7. governance and policy licensing (B4).

The strongest reusable negative results are non-transfer rules:

- memory does not transfer descent or lived participation;
- behavior does not transfer original obligation;
- descent does not transfer authority;
- local translation does not transfer global coherence;
- sampling does not manufacture an intrinsic event;
- proof validity does not guarantee behavioral or lived authenticity;
- path existence does not guarantee legitimate governance.
