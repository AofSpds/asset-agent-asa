# Held-out protocol battery paper evaluation — N09 through N12

## Evaluation discipline

- Authorized sources were the unsealed held-out battery, frozen candidate papers N09–N12, and the frozen `OPEN_N09_N12.md` evaluation only.
- Each candidate is unchanged. H01–H06, H08, and H09 start from the same semantic checkpoint `S0`, event ledger `L0`, and initial registry `R0`. Their metamorphic variants are isolated overlays and do not contaminate `S0`.
- H07 is one legal sequential Protocol Model mutation over unchanged `S0/L0`. H10 alone continues from H07's mutated `protocol:ack-gate/night` runtime revision; it does not inherit state from another case.
- H05's two inert registry subjects are case-local identifiers and add no Object Model semantics. H10's added sensor ping is observation-only. No action proposal has an executor.
- A described representation or safety rule is not an observed held-out response. The frozen papers contain no H01–H10 executable, installation/admission receipts, outputs, mutation histories, reset receipts, checkpoint digests, or post-case invariant audits. Every per-case behavioral outcome is therefore `NOT_PROVEN`.
- No outcome is upgraded to `SUPPORTED` from paper claims, and missing handlers are not repaired with evaluator-authored rules, adapters, manifests, schemas, interpreters, normalizers, or refusal mappings.
- `NOT_PROVEN` does not mean refusal, failure, falsehood, or out-of-scope. It means the required behavior was not executed or evidenced.
