## Layer A — `ARCHITECTURE_CONTACT`

Ordinal meanings:

- `STRONG` (`S`): a frozen, typed, finite mechanism directly contacts the metric and the relevant open/held-out stress exposes no material architectural omission on that surface.
- `MIXED` (`M`): an explicit mechanism exists, but a material boundary, ambiguity, missing operator, or incomplete transaction/state contract remains.
- `WEAK` (`W`): contact is mainly a generic carrier, adapter placeholder, opaque-storage path, or substantially incomplete mechanism.
- `FAIL` (`F`): the frozen architecture omits the surface or substitutes a materially different requirement.

These are paper-architecture judgments only.

## Evaluator calibration differences and normalization

The worker groups did not use `SUPPORTED`, `NOT_PROVEN`, and “observed” at the same evidence level. Raw outcome words therefore cannot be counted or compared directly.

| Evaluator group | Local calibration | Consequence before normalization |
|---|---|---|
| OPEN/HELDOUT N01–N04 | `SUPPORTED` was reserved for actual implementation evidence. Close paper fit remained `NOT_PROVEN`; incomplete mechanics were `PARTIAL`; missing evaluators were `OUT_OF_SCOPE`. | Architecture contact was embedded in mechanism prose rather than the outcome label. |
| OPEN/HELDOUT N05–N08 | `SUPPORTED` and `OBSERVED_AS_SPECIFIED` were used at explicitly stated “architecture semantics,” while implementation execution remained separately `NOT_PROVEN`. | Paper contact looked empirically stronger even though the execution layer matched the other groups. |
| OPEN/HELDOUT N09–N12 | No paper mechanism was upgraded. Every behavioral result remained `NOT_PROVEN`; notes described potential contact and missing lowerings only. | Architecture contact was intentionally under-expressed in the outcome labels. |

Normalization rule:

| Source wording | `ARCHITECTURE_CONTACT` treatment | `EXECUTION_EVIDENCE` treatment |
|---|---|---|
| `SUPPORTED` / `OBSERVED_AS_SPECIFIED` at architecture semantics | `STRONG` only when the necessary mechanism is frozen; otherwise `MIXED` or `WEAK` for evaluator-supplied lowering | `NOT_PROVEN` without an executable receipt |
| `NOT_PROVEN` with a concrete frozen mechanism | `STRONG`, `MIXED`, or `WEAK` according to specificity and battery-exposed gaps | `NOT_PROVEN` |
| `PARTIAL` | `MIXED` or `WEAK` | `NOT_PROVEN` |
| `OUT_OF_SCOPE` | `WEAK` or `FAIL` on the affected capability metric; honest scope handling can still be `STRONG` on M17 | `NOT_PROVEN` without an actual refusal receipt |
| Architecture-level `REFUSE` / “correctly refused” | Contact with M10/M17 only | `NOT_PROVEN` without an executed zero-effect receipt |

This is why N05–N08 receive no empirical credit for optimistic architecture-semantic labels, while N01–N04 and N09–N12 retain architecture contact where their workers deliberately kept behavioral outcomes `NOT_PROVEN`.

## Adequacy boundary

No candidate has demonstrated, against one unchanged executable implementation:

1. candidate-native load and canonical digest receipts for the common fixture;
2. complete open and held-out outputs with exact native envelopes;
3. exercised reset handles and final semantic-digest equality;
4. H07 legal Protocol-runtime mutation together with H08 atomic illegal-semantic refusal;
5. H09 plural action conflict without arbitration/execution and H10 stateful switch/revision behavior;
6. observed performance, resource, recovery, isolation, or failure-signature behavior; and
7. executable evidence for any M1–M18 cell.

Broad paper contact is not broad capability proof. N07 has comparatively uniform architecture contact but also the highest evaluator-lowering/overfit exposure; N10 has distinctive hybrid-dynamics contact; N06 has distinctive causal/counterfactual explanation; every family retains material complementary strengths and gaps. No candidate is a proven broadly capable single architecture. No candidate text is edited, no absent adapter/Protocol lowering is inferred, and no post-hoc successor is rescored as its frozen parent.
