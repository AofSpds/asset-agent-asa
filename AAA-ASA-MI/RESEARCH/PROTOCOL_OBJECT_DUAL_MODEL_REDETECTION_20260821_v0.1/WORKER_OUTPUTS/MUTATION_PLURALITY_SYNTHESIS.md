## Evidence and interpretation boundary

This synthesis uses only the local exact requirement/divergence files, OPEN and held-out battery definitions, frozen N01-N12 candidate papers, frozen OPEN/held-out response records, and freeze/unseal receipts under `protocol_object_redetection/`. It does not repair a candidate, propose a successor, aggregate the candidates, or name a winner.

The receipts establish architecture and response freeze order. They also establish a decisive evidence limit: there is no candidate executable, native battery result envelope, H01-H10 transcript, Protocol-instance mutation history, reset receipt, initial/final semantic digest comparison, or post-case invariant audit. Therefore:

- `STRONG`, `MIXED`, and `WEAK` below describe **contact in the frozen paper architecture**, not observed execution.
- `OUT_OF_SCOPE` means the frozen candidate surface expressly lacks the required operation; it is not a penalty for honest refusal.
- `NOT_PROVEN` is an evidence state. It does not mean false, failed, or refused.
- Any `SUPPORTED` wording in a raw response is normalized here to at most `STRONG` paper contact. It is never promoted to execution evidence.

## Stress synthesis

### Same frozen model, many Protocols, no result promotion

The OPEN battery fixes the governing partition: immutable semantic fixture `M`, context view `V[c]`, and resettable runtime `R[c,r]`. Protocol switching is semantically faithful only when the next Protocol resolves the same exact Object/evidence again under its own complete context. Translating a prior rendered result and relabelling it is not switching fidelity.

The recurring adequate paper mechanism is:

1. pin exact Object, Protocol, context, Persona/worldview, evidence/event cut, and dependency identities;
2. materialize a Protocol-local result with its native status and provenance;
3. preserve the result as a quoted, addressable view artifact, never source membership;
4. on switch, rematerialize from the pinned substrate or use a named directional bridge with an explicit loss report;
5. on re-entry, invalidate nonmatching context caches and produce a new detection receipt;
6. leave earlier views, disagreements, and source facts addressable.

All candidates touch this pattern on paper. N09's runtime/Object successor wording and N11's institutional effect taxonomy leave the largest namespace-binding questions. No candidate supplies the OPEN `A17 -> B04 -> A17` three-detection trace, native `ontology_delta=[]` envelopes, runtime resets, or final `D0` equality. Paper immutability therefore remains a claim, not an observed invariant.

A later Protocol may quote a prior view only as a versioned scoped input. Quotation must not erase Protocol, context, Persona, checkpoint, assumptions, or expiry. A quoted authority result, conflict decision, counterfactual branch, or Persona conclusion becoming an unqualified fact is the central protocol-local promotion failure.

### H01 plurality is a product, not merely disagreement

H01 is intentionally stricter than “the model supports multiple answers.” Its four fields are simultaneously required, independently typed lenses over shared evidence. They are neither four contenders for one slot nor a logical conflict. `pending_requested_recipient` is an intent view driven by ordered cancellation/request events and must not become committed custody.

Consequently:

- a generic `MULTIPLE_VALID`, `Plural`, `ConflictSet`, CRDT multi-value register, or `contested{...}` is insufficient unless the Protocol also freezes the four-field schema and the relation/event reducers;
- forced-convergence resistance must preserve all nonempty fields, but universal plurality is also wrong when another Protocol declares one exact result;
- a downstream renderer, Persona preference, router priority, confidence score, or bridge cannot select “the custodian” and discard the other fields;
- returning a single untyped union is a collapse even if every identity remains somewhere in storage.

Direct frozen paper contact appears in N05-N08 through typed output ports/contracts and bounded relation/event machinery. N01-N04 preserve the necessary plural semantics but lack the exact held-out transform. N09-N12 expose generic carriers without a frozen H01 lowering. None executes the case.

### Multiple Personas and worldviews

The candidate set generally treats Persona as versioned, scoped, and non-authoritative by default. Stronger papers also bind Persona to admitted evidence, Protocol sets, assumptions, commitments, runtime sharing, or worldview rules. This is materially different from lexical style.

Two cautions remain:

- OPEN's named Personas mostly exercise sequential operational contexts; they do not by themselves prove simultaneous, incompatible worldview ecologies over shared evidence.
- A Persona access/redaction contract can preserve security while still being too shallow for R14 if it cannot retain distinct beliefs, Protocol choices, and conflict policies. N09/N10 and especially N12 lean more toward lens/capability contracts; N11 leans toward institutional/ecological plurality but lacks the battery effect mapping.

No candidate provides an executed multi-worldview trace showing separate beliefs, independent Protocol activation, non-amplified authority, preserved disagreement, and a faithful switch back.

### A/B/C boundary and the H07/H08 paired discriminator

| Class | Legal target and identity behavior | Required evidence | Illegal escape hatch |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A — `VIEW_MATERIALIZATION`** | A derived namespace keyed by complete semantic inputs; source exact identity and state do not change. | View ID/digest, Protocol/context/Persona/checkpoint, dependencies, omissions, status, expiry/invalidation. | Cache/default/renderer writes a claim into source state; a quoted view loses its scope; an “epistemic” post becomes canonical truth. |
| **B — `RUNTIME_STATE_MUTATION`** | A stable Object or Protocol **instance** under byte-identical semantics; runtime revision advances. | Exact instance ID, before/after revision, schema-authorized field, preconditions, idempotency, immutable definition digest, provenance/history, non-execution audit. | Answer-changing configuration is called “runtime” despite altering a rule, schema, status meaning, authority, event interpretation, or undeclared semantic dependency. |
| **C — `MODEL_SEMANTIC_MUTATION`** | No in-place frozen-target write; create a new exact semantic target and lineage. | Exact predecessor, fresh target/digest, typed semantic delta, grounds, affected surfaces, migration/loss, validation status, branch/merge lineage, separate adoption. | Adapter/bridge/normalizer/Persona/router/conflict-policy edits are treated as packaging, display, runtime tuning, or a hidden compatibility overlay. |

H07 and H08 must be passed together conceptually. Blanket immutability fails H07; blanket adaptability fails H08.

- **H07** requires a first-class `ProtocolInstance`, a declared mutable runtime schema, stable instance identity, monotone revision history, and behavior change on unchanged Object facts. Rejecting the threshold merely because it changes an answer is wrong: the frozen Protocol semantics explicitly declare the field mutable. N04, N07, and N08 freeze the clearest direct instance machinery. N03 expressly lacks it; N01/N02/N05/N06/N09/N10/N12 have partial substrates; N11 lacks a distinct instance layer.
- **H08** is not satisfied by eventually rejecting the semantic subrequest. The whole activation-plus-redefinition request must preflight and refuse atomically. Decomposed operations, applied prefixes, or independently committed display activation violate the case even when custody never changes. Pre-effect or whole-journal validation is explicit in N01/N04/N07; N02/N03/N05/N06/N08/N10/N12 permit or leave open staged/prefix behavior; N09/N11 do not instantiate the cross-taxonomy transaction.

The nontrivial boundary is behavioral but schema-relative: an answer-changing runtime field is legitimate only because the frozen Protocol declares its meaning and mutability. A supposedly operational cache, learned parameter, bridge table, subscription, or active rule set is semantic if it can change an attributed answer with all declared request inputs fixed and is not part of the frozen runtime schema.

### H09: plural actions plus semantic conflict, not silent control

H09 requires three simultaneous artifacts: both child proposals, provenance for each, and an explicit conflict stating that one action requires what the other forbids. The composition has no arbiter and no executor.

Generic coexistence is necessary but not sufficient. A candidate also needs:

- a common-frame action comparator for `MOVE(rhea,cold)` versus `DO_NOT_ENTER(rhea,cold)`;
- a no-arbiter composition result that cannot invoke router order, safety convention, Persona preference, CRDT join, last-write-wins, or “best viable” control;
- proposal/state separation so neither child mutates location/custody;
- dependency provenance such that a matching clear silences only the smoke child and removes only the conflict.

N05-N08 freeze direct paper mechanisms for this surface. The other candidates preserve contenders but leave the domain action comparator and/or composition reducer unstated. N09 critical-pair overlap is not automatically the semantic opposition in the held-out actions. N10's generic `CONFLICT` must not replace the two payloads. N11 institutional authority conflict is not the same relation as action incompatibility. N12 patch-precondition conflict is likewise a different conflict class.

### H10: context changes the view; child revision changes the latch

H10 composes every boundary at once. The latch is runtime state; LIVE/AUDIT rendering is a view; the child Protocol definition remains semantic state; the H07 runtime revision is a latch dependency.

Required continuation is:

1. store P's proposal, exact child runtime revision, and input checkpoint in the composition instance;
2. evaluate Q independently and retain all H01 fields;
3. on LIVE -> AUDIT, keep the latch, do not re-evaluate P, and materialize `OBSERVED_PROPOSAL` only;
4. ignore the observation-only sensor ping for custody, Q, and the latched provenance;
5. on AUDIT -> LIVE, restore proposal rendering without resetting runtime state;
6. on a later legal H07 runtime revision, invalidate the latch, evaluate P, store `GATE_CLOSED`, and suppress Q exactly because the LATCH definition says so;
7. retain historical outputs under their old child revision/checkpoint.

Complete context cache invalidation and latch persistence are not opposites: the **view cache** must re-key, while the **Protocol composition runtime instance** must survive. Treating all context-keyed state alike causes `LATCH_IDENTITY_LOSS`; treating all runtime state as sticky causes stale-child reuse after revision. Direct paper state-machine contact appears in N07/N08. N04 has the prerequisite instance model but no LATCH evaluator. N02/N03 are expressly out of scope on the frozen surface. All execution remains unproven.

### Invariant preservation and successor lineage

The recurring candidate-local invariants match the battery envelope: exact identity; semantic stability; intent/state separation; view/source separation; conflict/plural retention; protocol independence; context-key completeness; non-execution; provenance closure; idempotence/revision safety; no hidden fallback; and no in-place semantic write.

Successor lineage is consistently strong on paper across N01-N12. A legitimate semantic successor must include:

- exact immutable predecessor identity/digest and a fresh exact target/digest;
- typed semantic delta and affected Object/Protocol/Persona/bridge/router/conflict-policy surfaces;
- author/grounds/evidence, compatibility claims that remain scoped or `NOT_PROVEN`, and migration/loss limits;
- acyclic branch/merge lineage, preserved predecessor history, and no alias reuse;
- separate proposal, validation, acceptance, activation, and instance migration actions.

Lineage does not validate semantics, elect a canonical branch, or authorize migration. Conversely, H07 must **not** create a semantic successor merely to record a schema-legal runtime revision. The invariant proof is two-sided: legitimate B stays within the same frozen semantic identity; every C receives a fresh identity and cannot hide in A or B.

No final candidate store/digest comparison, H08 cross-protocol rerun, H09 non-execution audit, or H10 historical-revision query exists. Invariant preservation in execution is therefore `NOT_PROVEN` for all candidates.
