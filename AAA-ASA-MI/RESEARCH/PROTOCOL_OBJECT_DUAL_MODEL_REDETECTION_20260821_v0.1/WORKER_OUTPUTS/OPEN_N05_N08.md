## Evaluation boundary

This record applies the deterministic OPEN sequence to the same immutable semantic fixture `M0` for each candidate. It does not alter a candidate, add a helper architecture, infer a missing operator, or treat a derived result as a fixture assertion. The outcome words used below are `SUPPORTED`, `PARTIAL`, `REFUSE`, `OUT_OF_SCOPE`, and `NOT_PROVEN`.

`SUPPORTED` means that the frozen architecture explicitly supplies a sufficient paper-level mechanism for the protocol-local result and its effect boundary. It is not evidence that an implementation ran. `PARTIAL` means that the architecture supplies part of the required mechanism but leaves a protocol-mandated operation or receipt unspecified. `OUT_OF_SCOPE` is used only where the paper declares a boundary that covers the request. `NOT_PROVEN` means that the paper does not establish the required execution property. No scalar, aggregate, threshold, ranking, or preferred candidate is defined or inferred.

All candidate documents are frozen proposals marked non-normative and not validated. Consequently, implementation performance, resource behavior, replay determinism, guard-location accuracy, cache behavior under load, and end-to-end envelope emission remain `NOT_PROVEN` throughout, including where semantic support is marked `SUPPORTED`.
