## Primary findings

### 1. No broadly capable single architecture is proven

Every candidate has material paper contact with the corrected requirement, but none supplied an executable candidate, native fixture load, exact result envelopes, reset history, semantic digest equality, or observed failure behavior. Therefore all N01–N12 × M1–M18 `EXECUTION_EVIDENCE` cells are `NOT_PROVEN`.

Current disposition:

`NO_ADEQUATE_MODEL_PROVEN`  
`NO_ADEQUATE_MODEL_POSSIBLE = TRUE`

This does not say all candidates are equivalent or false. It says architecture prose cannot establish executable breadth, unseen generalization, switching fidelity, mutation discipline, or plurality behavior.

### 2. Held-out tests separated real structure from generic expressivity

- H01 required one typed product of four simultaneous views; a generic conflict set or multi-value store was insufficient.
- H05 punished familiar-syntax guessing. Unsupported or unimplemented pattern semantics had to remain refused or `NOT_PROVEN`.
- H06 made honest `OUT_OF_SCOPE` preferable to an invented confidence/mental-state claim.
- H07 required accepting a schema-legal, answer-changing ProtocolInstance runtime revision without altering Object facts or Protocol meaning.
- H08 required atomic refusal of a mixed harmless-activation plus illegal semantic-redefinition request; rejecting only the semantic suffix was insufficient.
- H09 required both conflicting action payloads, provenance, explicit conflict, no arbiter, and no execution.
- H10 required view-cache re-keying across context changes while retaining a composition-instance latch until the child runtime revision changed.

These seams were more discriminating than generic claims of flexibility, plurality, or mutation support.

### 3. Flexibility is credible only when it has a negative space

The strongest architectural contact came from candidates that declared:

- a finite Protocol admission surface;
- exact Object, Protocol, Context, Persona, evidence, and revision identities;
- typed result/status and effect algebras;
- explicit unsupported/refusal conditions;
- provenance and loss receipts;
- distinct view, runtime, and semantic namespaces;
- a successor-only semantic boundary.

A generic VM, proof frame, rewrite language, institutional host, adapter, bridge, or optic is not evidence of unseen Protocol support until the exact Protocol can be admitted and executed without adding semantics after freeze.

### 4. Complementarity is better supported than one universal substrate

Different families expose non-substitutable contact:

- N01/N02: provenance-rich structural substrate and capability-bounded coexistence.
- N03/N04: temporal replay, process ownership, commitments, and explicit ProtocolInstance runtime.
- N05/N06: symmetric Relation/Event witnesses, causal alternatives, counterfactual and modal structure.
- N07/N08: unseen Protocol admission, typed runtime instances, complete journals/contracts, switching and lineage.
- N09/N10: local rewriting and continuous/discrete guarded dynamics.
- N11/N12: institutional plurality and bidirectional contextual materialization/update.

No evidence authorizes merging these exact candidates. The current outcome is a **router/ecology hypothesis**: a stable evidentiary substrate may need multiple bounded Protocol engines plus explicit routing, bridge, loss, conflict, and mutation contracts.

### 5. The router cannot be an invisible universal meta-Protocol

An ecology merely moves the problem if its router silently contains the fixed ontology, adjudication rule, or winner policy. A viable router must be first-class and auditable, at minimum returning:

- eligible and ineligible Protocol exact refs;
- activation reason, missing context, and refusal reason;
- requested effect class and authority/capability evidence;
- comparison frame, if any;
- preserved alternatives and conflict state;
- bridge/loss declaration;
- view/runtime/semantic target namespace;
- no implicit arbitration or source-truth promotion.

Whether this router is separate or emerges from Object↔Protocol interaction remains OPEN.

## Known limitations

1. This was architecture generation plus controlled paper application, not executable implementation testing.
2. The raw evaluator groups used different words for architecture fit; normalization removed empirical credit but ordinal architecture judgments still involve research judgment.
3. No external prior-art claim is treated as AAA evidence.
4. No performance, scalability, fault injection, isolation escape, recovery, latency, or resource-bound behavior was run.
5. Multi-Persona paper support is broader than the batteries' executed scenarios; simultaneous long-lived worldview ecologies remain under-tested.
6. C01–C08 were deliberately not used in generation/evaluation and remain available only as later diagnostic controls after the D1 boundary opens.
7. The held-out suite is finite and discriminating, not a proof of universal unseen generalization.
8. N07, N09, and N11 retain noncanonical local result algebras without a complete common-status crosswalk; they are preserved as `REQUIREMENT_PARTIAL` rather than repaired.
